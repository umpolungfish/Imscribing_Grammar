\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{\large 0 -- \emph{Prima Materia}.}

{\normalsize Performing the operations on ordinary, inert matter was an understandable, categorical error of history's alchemists. However some, like Luria, knew \emph{precisely} the material upon which the \emph{Work} is meant to be done.}

\emph{Take}: \textbf{abstract category $\mathcal{C}$.}

No primitives yet --- the starting object has no invariants beyond the axioms of a category itself. This was always the intended \emph{prima materia}: the undifferentiated substance from which begins the \emph{Work}.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{\large I -- \emph{Nigredo}} (Logical on $\mathcal{C}$).
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item L1 (adjunction) $\to$ $R$ (relational mode: adjoint structure)
  \item L2 (dagger) $\to$ $R$ (confirms and refines $R$ value)
\end{itemize}

$R$ is the first primitive to crystallize: it is a logical property of the morphisms of $\mathcal{C}$ directly.\newline
\textit{This is the \emph{nigredo}: the blackening, decomposing the undifferentiated into distinct relational modes.}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{\large II -- \emph{Albedo}} (Inductive on $\mathcal{C}$).
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item I2 (iteration) $\to$ $H$ (temporal depth: depth of stabilization)
  \item I3 (classifying) $\to$ $\Omega$ (winding: $\pi_1$ of $B\mathcal{C}$)
  \item I4 (Yoneda) $\to$ $D$ (dimensionality: $D_\odot$ if Yoneda is point-surjective; free cocompletion size gives $D_\infty$, $D_\triangle$, $D_\wedge$)
\end{itemize}

\textit{This is the \emph{albedo}: the whitening, purifying the base substance and reconstituting it at higher resolution.}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{\large III -- \emph{Citrinitas}} (Algebraic on $\mathcal{C}$).
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item A1 (monoidal) $\to$ $S$ (stoichiometry: arity of generators); $P$ (parity: symmetry of tensor)
  \item A2 (monad) $\to$ $K$ (kinetics: spectral properties of $\mu$)
  \item A3 (dagger) $\to$ $F$ (fidelity: unitality of duals)
  \item A4 (enriched) $\to$ $\Gamma$ (interaction grammar: enrichment base)
\end{itemize}

After Stage~3, $\mathcal{C}$ is a symmetric monoidal dagger category with a monad and enrichment. All shape primitives are determined, but the gate primitives ($T$, $\Phi$) require one more step.\newline
\textit{This is the \emph{citrinitas}: the yellowing, transmuting intermediate forms into structured algebraic layers.}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{\large IV -- \emph{Rubedo}} (Logical on algebraically-enriched $\mathcal{C}$).
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item L3 (cartesian closure) $\to$ $T$ (topology: does the state space admit internal homs?)
  \item L6 (paraconsistent negation) $\to$ determines the truth-value structure in which L4 will be evaluated
  \item L4 (Lawvere) under L6 $\to$ $\Phi$:
  \begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1em]
    \item[\small $\circ$] Classical (L4 holds, no L6): $\Phi_c$
    \item[\small $\circ$] Dialetheic (LP truth value $\mathbf{b}$): $\Phi_c^\mathbb{C}$
    \item[\small $\circ$] Inclosure (contradicts via Inclosure Schema, L6 blocks explosion): $\Phi_\text{EP}$
    \item[\small $\circ$] L4 fails: $\Phi_\text{sub}$ or $\Phi_\text{super}$
  \end{itemize}
  \item L5 (Frobenius) $\to$ $P$ promotes to $P_{\pm}^{\text{sym}}$ if $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$
\end{itemize}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Cross-stage: $G$ (\emph{Albedo} $\times$ \emph{Citrinitas}).}
$G$ (scope) is the correlation length of the Yoneda embedding under the monoidal structure. Local ($G_\beth$) if the tensor decouples; global ($G_\aleph$) if faithfulness holds across all scales.
\par\medskip
\textit{This is the \emph{rubedo}: the reddening, the final perfection where the gate primitives lock.}

\bigskip
\textit{Solvatum et Praecipitatum --- the gate primitives lock.}
