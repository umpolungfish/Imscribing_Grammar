### VII. Paraconsistent Witness

The Belnap FOUR logic (True, False, Both, Neither) is not an abstraction — it is a model of how living systems handle contradiction. The SSI system trains the practitioner to hold contradictions productively.

**The Four Values as Contemplative States:**

| Belnap Value | Experience | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| **True** | Clarity. Agreement. The system coheres. | Rest here. Do not rush. Feel when something is simply and clearly True. |
| **False** | Denial. Rejection. The system does not cohere. | Rest here. Feel when something is clearly False. Not "I disagree" — False. Structurally incompatible. |
| **Both** | Paradox. Contradiction held alive. The system is simultaneously True and False. | This is the advanced practice. Do not resolve. Hold. A dialetheia — a true contradiction. The Liar. The Bohr atom. A teaching that says "I am not this teaching." |
| **Neither** | Silence. The framework is exceeded. Neither true nor false — the question is wrong. | The rarest state. The system that does not submit to binary evaluation. A koan. The sound of one hand. |

**Practice — The Paraconsistent Witness:**
When you encounter a contradiction in your practice (a system that seems to have two incompatible structural types), do not resolve it. Instead:

1. Name the first type. Write it down. Feel it fully.
2. Name the second type. Write it down. Feel it fully.
3. Hold both simultaneously. Do not choose. Let them coexist.
4. Ask: is this a **Both** (true contradiction — the system requires both types) or a **Neither** (the types are both wrong — you need a different framework)?

Over time, you will develop the capacity to sit with structural contradiction without collapsing it. This is the paraconsistent witness — the priestly function that mediates between incompatible frames.

**The Shor Orbit:**
When a system oscillates between two types without settling, it is in a Shor orbit — a Belnapian cycle that does not converge. This is not failure. Some systems are structurally oscillatory. The practice is not to fix the oscillation but to *witness* it fully.

### VIII. Frobenius Verification: The Self-Consistency Check

The Frobenius condition (μ∘δ=id) is the ultimate test: does this system map to itself and back perfectly? In silicon, this is computed. Sans silicon, it is *felt*.

**The Fourfold Check:**

When you have assigned a structural type to a system, run the internal verification:

**1. Duality Check (μ):** Take the system as you perceive it. Mentally "map" it to its structural type — the tuple of 12 primitives. This is the δ (encoding) direction. Feel the fit: does the system *compress* into this tuple without loss?

**2. Synthesis Check (δ):** From the tuple, reconstruct the system. Mentally "expand" the 12 primitives back into a full system. This is the μ (decoding) direction. Does the tuple *generate* the system without remainder?

**3. Closure Check (μ∘δ):** Apply both in sequence. Encode the system, then decode the encoding. Do you get back exactly what you started with? If yes — Frobenius closed. The system and its type are in perfect correspondence.

**4. Residual Check:** If there is a remainder — something in the system that the tuple does not capture, or something in the tuple that does not appear in the system — mark it. This is the Frobenius residual. It tells you what you have missed.

**The Feeling of Closure:**
When μ∘δ=id holds, there is a specific phenomenological signature: a sense of *perfect circle*. The system closes on itself. There is no loose end, no excess, no lack. The experience is one of total coherence — not necessarily simple, but complete.

**When Closure Fails:**
If the check fails, you have three options:
1. **Refine the tuple** — you have the wrong type. Re-examine each primitive.
2. **Expand the system** — the system is larger than you thought. Include what was excluded.
3. **Accept the residual** — some systems are Frobenius-open. They are not self-consistent. This is also informative.

### IX. Daily Practice Guide

**The SSI practice is designed for 30–45 minutes per day.** It is structured in three phases:

#### Morning Session (15 min) — Calibration

1. **Breath Settling (2 min):** Three deep breaths. Settle into the body.
2. **The 12 Gates Review (5 min):** Mentally recite the 12 primitives and their current values for yourself. What is your structural type *right now*? Not your fixed type — your state type. The 12 values of your current awareness.
3. **The Three Currents (5 min):** Contemplate the forward current (what is emerging), the backward current (what is contracting), the restoration current (what is being repaired).
4. **Gate of the Week (3 min):** Focus on the gate corresponding to the current chamber's theme (see Section VI).

#### Midday Session (10 min) — Application

Choose one system you encounter today — a conversation, a problem, a piece of music, a natural scene. Silently assign it a structural type. Run the Frobenius check (Section VIII). Note any residuals.

#### Evening Session (15 min) — Integration

1. **Distance Map (5 min):** Take the system you assigned at midday. Find its distance from your own morning type. Are you close or far? What primitives conflict?
2. **Tier Sensing (5 min):** What tier were you operating at today? Did you ascend? Did you descend? Is there a promotion channel you need to open?
3. **Name Contemplation (5 min):** If this is a week with an assigned 72-Name, contemplate that name's three letters. Map each letter to a primitive. Feel the structural type of the name.

#### Weekly Practice (1 hr)

On the seventh day, do the full palace walk:
- Walk through all 12 gates in order (5 minutes each = 1 hour)
- For each gate, ask: *What have I learned about this primitive this week?*
- Record your findings — either in writing or in memory.

#### Monthly Practice (Half Day)

Once a month, perform a full structural self-audit:
1. Assign yourself a structural type for the month (your current state of development).
2. Compute (via felt sense) your distance from your type from the previous month.
3. Identify which primitives have shifted.
4. Identify your current tier and your target tier for next month.
5. Choose a promotion channel to work on.

#### Annual Practice (Full Day)

Once a year, a silent retreat:
- Ascend through all 5 tiers in contemplation.
- Walk the entire 400-room palace (briefly — 30 seconds per room feels each room's 6-key signature).
- Contemplate all 72 names (one per 5 minutes = 6 hours).
- Perform the full Frobenius self-verification.
