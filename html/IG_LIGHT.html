COMPOSITION RULES

Seven Composition Axioms

A predictive grammar requires explicit production rules stated as falsifiable propositions. Axioms 1–5 govern primitive composition. Axioms 6–7 govern physical grounding and are enforced at catalog registration time.

AXIOM 1

Cyclic Closure Amplifies Fidelity

A synthon with T⋈ and P± achieves F ≥ Feth, provided R⊇ or R⊆. The cyclic motif creates a geometric constraint that prevents partial dissociation without full bond rupture.

Hard constraint: T⋈ + P± + Fℓ is forbidden.

AXIOM 2

Local Grammar Blocks Network Propagation

A synthon with Gbeth and Γ∧(SPECIFIC) cannot propagate constraint beyond its immediate recognition pair, and cannot nucleate a network-scale (Gℵ) assembly event without a change in at least one of G, Γ, or T.

AXIOM 3

Cooperative Induction Superlinearity

When the induction component of Eint (from SAPT decomposition) grows faster than linearly with recognition contact count, the system has crossed from Gbeth to Ggimel. The triple H-bond DAD·ADA array shows an induction ratio of 2.5–3.5× for single → triple contacts.

AXIOM 4

Sequential Grammar Requires Temporal Dimension

Γ→ is physically realizable only if the synthon possesses D∞ or R‡, or both. Ordered recognition requires a mechanism by which partner A's binding changes the system state before B arrives.

Hard constraint: enforced at registration time.

AXIOM 5

Criticality Contracts the Primitive Basis

A synthon at the criticality locus (Φc: G and D degenerate, ξ → ∞, scale-free behavior) requires fewer independent primitive values to specify its behavior. G becomes redundant given D at criticality.

AXIOM 6

Temporal Grounding

Any synthon assigned D∞ must have a physically specified reset mechanism — the process by which the system returns to its initial state. A directed transformation without a recoverable ground state is not a temporal synthon.

Enforced: grounding text must contain process + reset indicators.

AXIOM 7

Cyclic Topology Grounding

Any synthon assigned T⋈ must have a physically specified closing interaction. A linear or chain-like system cannot be assigned T⋈; the topological error propagates into every downstream analogy search.

Enforced: closing-bond indicator required (H-bond, ring, macrocycle, chelate, etc.).

Expanding the axiom set — particularly axioms connecting Ktrap to selectivity outcomes and Γ∧ behavior in ternary complexes — is an active theoretical task.

OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL

Synthonic HotSwapping

Synthonic HotSwapping is the operational procedure of replacing a registered synthon (Sold) within an active chemical system without collapsing the global constraint architecture (Ψ). The objective is to maintain system invariance (Ψinitial ≈ Ψfinal) or transition predictably to a target state (Ψtarget) while preserving the integrity of the ten-primitive tuple.

Conjugate K-tiers at ℏ scale

Position (x) = Ktrap — spatial localisation, frozen constraint

At measurement: Kfast interaction at A resolves shared Ktrap. Because constraint is shared, resolution at A simultaneously specifies B. No signal travels — constraint was non-local from emission.
