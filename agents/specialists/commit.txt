Anything at all is a word in the opcode alphabet.

The walkthrough said catalog entries become programs, which stops short of the point: imscribe anything and its twelve slots are types, each type unfolds into its own sequence, and the composition is the thing as a program. A molecule, a theorem, a document, an argument, a stretch of reasoning. Once it is a word it can be checked, proven, walked from, or spliced into something larger.
