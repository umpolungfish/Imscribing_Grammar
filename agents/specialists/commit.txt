Give the math specialist the quantum surfaces in its prompt

Five distinct quantum surfaces exist and they are not variants of one engine: the
mOMonadOS Fibonacci anyon QC in Rust, the m3iosis Python mirror, the grammar tools that
dispatch into the kernel, the exact state-vector and MPS simulators under navigators, and
ParaASM's Belnap FOUR VM. The manifest named the kernel but the detail sat behind a
file_read, which is one call an agent has to decide to make before it knows the surface
exists.

The list is now in the math accent, which is inlined verbatim, so the commands are in the
prompt itself. Two failure modes go with it, both found by running into them:
fusion_space_dimension is the vacuum sector, so at three strands the representation is
1x1 and its non-Abelian invariants describe a trivial matrix; and sampling braid words is
not searching, since a long word is a near-random state and loses to Haar sampling on
both best and mean.

ig-docs/quantum_computation_tools.md carries the full reference, including the capacity
table — 19 strands is the first fusion space that holds d=2048, at 2584 dimensions.
