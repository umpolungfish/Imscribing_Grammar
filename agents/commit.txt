Hyperdimensional IMASM gematria, on the ring.

177 coordinates over a word, every one of them verified to survive rotation
rather than asserted to.

  opcode census      12   the opcodes, order discarded
  ring transitions  144   ordered pairs with the closing edge included
  landing spectrum   16   how many of the n cuts land in each register
  scalars                 length, pairs, greatest depth, total ordinal

A word is a ring, so a coordinate read from absolute position measures where
the word was cut and not the word: rows of a matrix, tiers of a tetraktys, odd
against even. One rotation moves every value into a different row. Each block
here is a multiset over the whole word, a count on the ring, or an aggregate
over the entire orbit, and each is checked with rotation_invariant.

The landing spectrum is the coordinate that belongs to the ring rather than to
a word. The landing of any single cut is the phase-bearing quantity every ROTAT
audit reports; the distribution over all cuts is not, and two words agreeing on
census and transitions can still put their cuts in different registers.

The check earned itself immediately: the scalar block failed on first run.
`pairs` and `max_depth` came from a linear depth scan, so a rotation cutting
through a region counted an unmatched fuse followed by an unmatched split and
both moved. They are read as a loop now, from a start where the stack never
underflows, which the cycle lemma provides whenever splits and fuses balance.

Deposits are reported outside the signature: a rotation moves the IFIX and
everything after a fixation is inert, so the deposit count belongs to the cut.

Reachable as imasm_gematria or hyper_gematria. 54 grammar tools, registry 34 of
34, deep check green.
