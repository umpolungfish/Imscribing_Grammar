The specialists take -i, and the model comes from the environment.

The launchers had no -i at all, so the interactive aliases died on an unrecognized argument the moment they pointed here. They also carried a hardcoded grok fallback, which meant anything running without the environment loaded silently used a model nobody had chosen and billed for it; the model now comes from IG_PROVIDER and IG_MODEL, resolved after parsing so --model can still stand in, and it says so plainly when neither is set.
