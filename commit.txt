additive_spectrum: representation counts as a winding spectrum.

r_A(n) is the n-th coefficient of f(z)^2 where f(z) = sum over a in A of z^a. On
the unit circle f is a function of a WINDING, so evaluating the generating
function is evaluating at a rational number of turns and r is recovered from the
root-of-unity lattice exactly.

This is the move shared_floor named as the gap between the proved counting bound
and the proven attractor: criticality to complex plane. It is the same move
Erdos-Fuchs makes past Erdos-Turan's bound, and the coordinate on the circle is
the winding.

The recovery is checked against a direct count on every call rather than
assumed, which caught the bug on the first run: |f(z)|^2 is the DIFFERENCE
function and f(z)^2 is the sum function, and extracting r from the former
recovers a different sequence entirely. It reported exact=False and the tool
would have been wrong and plausible without it.

What the spectrum shows: bounded r is flatness. Strict Sidon sets sit at
min/max around 0.19, sets with r reaching 8 sit at 0.0002 to 0.004. So the
conjecture reads as a compatibility question between being a basis and being
flat, and that is finitely searchable. A randomized greedy over windows of 20,
40 and 60 at max_r <= 2 covers 0.71, 0.61 then 0.49 of the window, and at
max_r <= 4 covers 0.95, 0.90 then 0.87. Falling in N at fixed C and rising in C
at fixed N, which is the conjecture's shape. Evidence, not proof: a greedy gives
lower bounds and the trend could be the search rather than the mathematics.

sidon_check is the STRICT condition, at most two ordered representations, which
is what sidon_reps_le_two proves in p4ramill. Mian-Chowla satisfies only the
weaker i < j version and fails this one, so the name is spelled out rather than
left to collide.
