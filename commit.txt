Stop truncating the persisted description at 300 characters

The catalog's description field is the only prose it carries, and 675 entries sit
at exactly 300 characters with 805 ending mid-word. belnap_multilattice_sic_povm
stops at "CONFLICT RESOLUTION: all 5 " and sic_povm_bridge_monadic at "The bridge
mecha" — the resolution of those five conflicts and the bridge mechanism are lost
text, not unwritten text.

The cause is a one-line slice in the imscribe_system emit path, with a comment
that names the real concern: the model often dumps the whole task into the
description, and a verbose description wastes tokens in every subsequent
winding's context. That is true of the ECHO. The slice was applied to the value
handed to the dispatcher, so a context-economy measure was destroying the record
to save tokens in a transcript.

Now the dispatcher gets the whole description and _emit_short_echo trims only
what re-enters context, marking the cut with the full stored length so the
shortening is visible rather than silent. Fixed in both live agents,
true_agentic_agent and odot_operator, which write the same canonical catalog.

Not touched: true_agentic_agent1 and true_agentic_agent2 are byte-identical
snapshots that nothing in the tree invokes, and the two website copies under
imscribesite_repo and imscribe.com write a different catalog and live in other
repos. Their copies of the line are recorded here rather than changed.

This does not recover the 805 already-cut descriptions.
