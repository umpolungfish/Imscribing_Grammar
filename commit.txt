Finish the mark conversion, and correct two documents

The code conversion was done in three passes and each missed a class the previous
filter could not see. This closes it: markdown that teaches the alphabet, the
bare ixcriptions catalog, and every generated artifact under digital/ now read in
the marks, and nothing in any repo still binds AFWD to > or AREV to <.

Two documents carried real errors, both found by checking them rather than
reading them.

master_methods_d2048_stark reported the moduli field as 2^20 over F and 2^21 over
Q, and marked the 2^26 reading FALSIFIED. Those are the oo-unramified numbers,
which the conductor paper two days later identifies as the retracted error. The
degrees are now 2^21 over F and 2^22 over Q, at p_2^12 oo_1, with the ray class
group 2^27 of type [4096,1024,8,4].

That document and alt_sic_moduli_conductor both carry an exponent vector [-1,3,2]
and they are not the same element: one is over eps_fund and the uniformizers of
the degree-1024 tower with norm 2^56, the other over eps_d and g_-, g_+ inside F
with norm -35905737691441125, which is odd. Since m_d = 5 (mod 8) and m_d is odd,
2 is inert AND unramified in F, so F contains no such uniformizers at all. A
shared numeral is not a shared object; the collision is now named in both places.

sic_fiducial_extraction_2part_bypass stated the minimal polynomial as
x^2-2047x-1, which evaluates to -2. It is x^2-2047x+1, as its own verification
line said and as Proposition 7.1 gives. It also equated Q(sqrt3,sqrt5,sqrt409,
sqrt683) with Q(sqrt m_d), degree 16 against degree 2, and gave the rational
primes 3 and 5 norms 3 and 5 rather than 9 and 25.

moduli_exact.txt now says what it is: ten of its twelve rows are 642-digit lindep
fits at 2000 working digits, genuine only at z0 and z6, and its SUM cannot be 1
because it sums fits. The height test that catches this is STATE.md's own and the
file had never been put through it.

d2048_sieve.rs and sunit_recognition_d2048.py now record that their five
generators satisfy g3^2 = (d-3)eps and g4^2 = (d+1)eps, so an exponent vector over
them is not a unique name, and at d=2048 the list cannot generate the S-unit group
because 409 and 683 are forced in without generators.
