closure_operator: measurement and fixed points as one subject

The ninth ⊙perator, for the cluster that ovm, oneshots, ctc and nesting make
between them.

They are one cluster and not four commands. The d=12 SIC fiducial is stationary
under the Zauner element, so nesting it in the action carrying that symmetry
closes in one shot — the apparatus coincides with what it measures. A
measurement that reconstructs its input is mu-delta=id wearing measurement
clothing, and a fixed point that survives its own operator is a measurement that
lost nothing. Split them and you prove the same thing twice under two names,
which is what a specialist for each would have produced.

The prompt carries the rule those commands serve — the three classes and the
fourth that was added, and the conservative/dissipative distinction that decides
which classes a domain can populate at all. That distinction is why a flat
reading is not an absent reading: a whole parameter range in the one-shot class
with nothing in a basin is what a conservative system looks like, not what a
broken instrument looks like.

Price is a commitment, not a nicety. One-shot costs nothing, iterated costs
steps, manufactured costs the width it smeared, and "it closed" without one of
those four is half an answer. The conformance check enforces it: "price" is in
the marker list beside the four commands and the four class names, because a
closure reported without its cost is the failure mode this specialist exists to
prevent.

It also states what does not exist. Postselection is nowhere in the kernel,
closed timelike curves appear once in the constellation as a catalog description
string, and ctc is a fixed-point-imposing machine over Belnap FOUR rather than a
simulation of spacetime. Better said plainly than discovered by someone acting
on the name.
