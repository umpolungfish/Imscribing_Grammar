The package was ignoring itself

`.gitignore` carried `imscrbgrmr/` under the virtual-environments heading. The
package is not an environment, it is the code: `algebra.py`, `models.py`, `cli.py`,
everything `imscribe` runs. The files that predate the rule stayed tracked, since
an ignore rule does not reach a tracked file, so nothing looked wrong. What the
rule did was swallow every file added after it. `git add .` skipped them without a
word and only `-f` would take one.

Eleven had gone that way, and they are not peripheral. `canonical_primitives.py`
is the module whose own docstring calls it the single source of truth for the
twelve primitives, and three modules import it, including the algebra as of the
change before this one. `proof_path/` is a whole subpackage. `vocal.py`,
`lambda_engine.py`, `canonical_algebra.py`, `agent_catalog.json` likewise. A fresh
clone could not import the algebra at all.

They are tracked now and the rule is gone, with a note in its place saying what it
was and why it is not coming back. `.venv/` and `venv/` were already listed twelve
lines further down, which is what that heading was for.

Two files stay untracked on purpose: `models.py.pre_shavian` and
`symbolic.py.fixed` are copies kept beside the originals, not sources. They are
still on disk.
