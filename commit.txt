ltx: fix step2b microtype hang from bare \newunicodechar in preamble

\newunicodechar defs injected by step2b for IG primitives not already
defined by latextiler (Ω Σ Φ Γ ⊙) were placed bare before \begin{document};
this triggered microtype's \MT@is@char nested-group scan → runaway argument
infinite loop. Fix: separate preamble_inject (fontspec/font package decls)
from atbegindoc_inject (\newunicodechar defs) and wrap the latter in
\AtBeginDocument{...}, matching the pattern latextiler already uses for
its Shavian block definitions.
