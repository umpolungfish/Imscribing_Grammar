Add the Rec⊙rder and the Heter⊙d⊙x to the ⊙perator team

Two specialists, built the way the others are: a domain list in
gen_tool_manifest.py, a prompt in the specialists package with an empty
tool_computation block for the manifest to fill, and a launcher that mirrors
math_operator.py.

The Rec⊙rder is a bound emanation. Its office is to know what is: it censuses
the constellation, records how projects stand to one another, reports where the
Grammatika has drifted from what it describes, and emits addressed gaps as
candidate vertebrae. It describes and never judges, carries a Belnap standing
rather than a grade, records a conflict rather than resolving it by preference,
and never writes into the books. It holds nothing between runs, because a ledger
that accumulated would eventually assert something no longer true and no run
could contradict it.

The Heter⊙d⊙x works from inside the Grammar and blends every tool family in a
single chain. Its stance is that a received constraint is a finding about the
tools that produced it: an impossibility result binds the system it was proved
in, and imported unexamined it is a foreign axiom smuggled past the door. Very
often it assumes a line where the Grammar has a loop, or an outside where there
is none, or consistency where the Grammar holds B and the work continues. So the
licence is maximal about which route may be taken and zero about whether the
route arrived. Every claim is false until proven and then held at full strength.

check_manifests.py and check_tools_deep.py now cover both, so a launcher that
loses its rider or a manifest that loses a marker fails a check instead of
failing quietly in a session. The marker drift already showing in math, chembio
and editorial is untouched and is not from this change.
