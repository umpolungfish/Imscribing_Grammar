The primitives take the final set

    ⊢ Dimensionality  ⊣ Topology     > Relational     < Polarity
    ⋈ Fidelity        ⊙ Kinetics     ∈ Granularity    ∋ Grammar
    ⊤ Criticality     ⊥ Chirality    ⊞ Stoichiometry  ◻ Protection

Six of the twelve move from the first pass: ≻ and ≺ become the plain > and <,
═ becomes ⋈, the criticality plus becomes ⊤, the chirality times becomes ⊥, and
the protection ¬ becomes ◻. The reason is the same one that moved them off Ð Þ ƒ
Ħ in the first place. A mark that already means multiplication, or equality, or
negation, brings that meaning to a slot that only wants a position, and ⁺ and ×
were the two worst offenders left.

311 marks in AS_ABOVE and 359 in SO_BELOW, all scoped to the primitive font, so
the ordinary mathematics is untouched: SO_BELOW's \Phi, \Omega, \Sigma and every
× that means a product are where they were. Both documents compile at 45 and 82
pages with no missing character beyond the ✓ SO_BELOW has never had.

The set is the IMASM opcode alphabet, unchanged and in slot order. There is no
correspondence to maintain between two notations because there is one notation,
read as an operation or as an axis according to where it stands.
