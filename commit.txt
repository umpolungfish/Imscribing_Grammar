Jones polynomial at an arbitrary root, so the level is a parameter

The kernel's fibqc jones is pinned at the Fibonacci root, t = 1/5 of a winding, so every
value it returns lies in Q(zeta_5) with phases on the tenths lattice. For the d=2048 SIC
discriminant 4190205 = 3*5*409*683 that supplies one prime of four, which is exactly why
the QC route delivered the sqrt5 part and stopped there. Stating that as a limit of the
method was wrong; it is a limit of one evaluation point.

scripts/jones_at_root.py computes the Kauffman bracket as an exact Laurent polynomial by
state sum, converts to the Jones polynomial in t, and substitutes a root only at the end,
so the level is an argument. quadratic_root_level(m) reports the conductor of Q(sqrt m),
which is the level at which that square root becomes reachable: 5 for sqrt5, 12 for
sqrt3, 409 for sqrt409, 2732 for sqrt683, and 4190205 for the whole discriminant.

Verified against five known Jones polynomials: unknot, both trefoil chiralities,
figure-eight, cinquefoil. At level 10, the kernel's own phase lattice, the trefoil gives
|V| = 1.618034.

Three bugs were found by that self-test and are worth recording, since each produced
plausible output. The identity smoothing was written as a strand swap, which models the
crossing as a permutation rather than as a smoothed diagram. The union-find allocated 2n
nodes where n are used, and the unused half counted as extra loops. And the geometric
smoothing was tied to the A/B label, which put the A-power out by two on every negative
crossing — visible only as a uniform half-integer shift in the mirror trefoil.
