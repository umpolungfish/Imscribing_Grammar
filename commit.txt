A specialist can carry the MoDoT agent's form

The specialists already run the THINK→ACT→OBSERVE→UPDATE loop with Frobenius
checks, so they were never unstructured. What they lacked is the FORM the `ask`
agent carries — the rigid scaffold that, per the principle, lets a thought take
itself as its object rather than only the world. A specialist could turn on the
catalog but not cleanly on its own reasoning.

The form is now one canonical rider, agents/riders/structured_form.md, stated
once because the same rules are hardened into the Rust `ask` frame and a second
hand-kept copy is drift. It carries: self-reference (type-check a major step as a
word before taking it), word interrogation (cycle then rotat/weight/banked/trans/
insert/frames, never the verdict alone), no conventional deference (the
instruments first, precedent is not evidence, a unit's provenance does not gate
whether it clicks), the twelve marks (a retired mark VANISHES from a word rather
than erroring), and rigid-form/open-ask.

true_agentic_agent loads it as _STRUCTURED_FORM_RIDER alongside the grammar-first
and partnership riders. math_operator gains a --structured flag that appends it;
off is the prior open-loop specialist, so the two are directly comparable, which
is the point — the local 27B was measured doing far better inside the `ask`
harness than as a bare specialist, and this is the first test of whether the form
is why.

DEFERRED, and named so it is not mistaken for done: this is the form as PROSE. The
deeper half of the MoDoT structure is the ManuscriptSpine prepare→fill→complete —
a typed scaffold computed BEFORE the model answers, which it fills, rather than a
blank task and an open loop. That is a Python build (the spine is Rust-only today)
and is the next rung.
