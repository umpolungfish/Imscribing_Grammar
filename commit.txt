Compute the RG flow table instead of typing it

The flow table in ig-docs/rg_running_grammar_sm.md rebuilt alpha_em from the gauge
couplings as alpha_em^-1 = alpha_2^-1 + (3/5) alpha_1^-1. alpha_1 is the GUT-normalised
hypercharge coupling, alpha_1 = (5/3) alpha_Y, so recovering alpha_Y^-1 multiplies by 5/3.
Dividing instead put sin^2(theta_W) at 0.455 at M_Z, and the document said 0.231 four
sections later without noticing.

scripts/rg_running.py runs the one loop from the PDG values at M_Z and prints the table.
Everything it needs is measured input; nothing in it is transcribed.

Correcting the factor moves three things. sin^2(theta_W) at M_Z comes out at 0.2312,
which is the PDG number the table was seeded with, so the reconstruction now closes and
the 3/13 match the document leads with is no longer contradicted by its own table. The UV
value goes to 0.3605 from 0.610 — still far from 3/13, so the conclusion that the ratio is
an infrared statement survives. And alpha_s/alpha_em at a single scale passes through 4 at
1.167e10 GeV, so gear = 4 has a same-scale reading and does not have to be bridged across
regimes.

The 1.033e12 GeV scale is unchanged. It comes from pure QCD running against alpha_em at
zero momentum, which the factor never touched.
