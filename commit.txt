Load a local model across both cards, and put device selection in one place

The transformers loader picked the single card with the most VRAM free and put
the whole model on it. Now it offers every card IG_DEVICES names the memory that
card actually has free right now, less a working reserve, and lets accelerate
balance the model across them — so a model too large for either card alone loads,
and a card already busy with a display gets a smaller share instead of OOMing
halfway through. One card still means one card. Verified end to end: Qwen3-1.7B
split 123 tensors onto cuda:0 and 188 onto cuda:1 and generated.

framework/ig_devices.py is now the only place that reads IG_DEVICES here, and the
navigators, the boundary-operator trainer and the GrammaFormer trainer all take
their device from it. A training run that fits one card does not want to be
split: the way both cards work at once is two runs, one pinned to each, which is
what a pinned IG_DEVICES gives. data_parallel() is there for the other case,
where the model fits and the throughput is the constraint.

Env: IG_MODEL and IG_PROVIDER are the names, IG_LOCAL_MODEL_DIR the weights,
IG_LOCAL_BASE_URL the local server. The old spellings still read.
