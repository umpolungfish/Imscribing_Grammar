  ParaEngine extends UniversalEngine with Belnap FOUR belief tracking.
  Flux and Belnap state kept in sync per register:
    Void('00') ↔ N   True('01') ↔ T   False('10') ↔ F   Both('11') ↔ B

  3-instruction kernel (circular PC → infinite):
    ENGAGR %r0          seed: force B on root register
    FSPLIT %r0 %r1 %r2  δ: co-multiply — two B-children
    FFUSE  %r1 %r2 %r0  μ: multiply — fold back into root

  Loop invariant: μ∘δ = id (Frobenius identity)
  All 3 registers stabilize at B permanently; paradox count grows without bound.
  ~200k steps/sec; live ANSI display updates every 30k steps.
  Ctrl+C for final summary.

  Usage: python3 para_loop.py
