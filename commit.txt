crossover_cli: quantum advantage certification, standalone

Packages the quantum_tnn.py crossover metric as its own CLI for the Fibonacci
anyon pipeline. Takes strands and depth with a gate string, or a braid word
directly, or --certify. The boundary is t_gate × n_gates × ε_2q > 0.1: above it
the circuit is classically simulable, below it the hardware certifies advantage.
