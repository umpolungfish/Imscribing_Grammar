imasm tool: write and derive, not just the instruments

The rider names `imasm write tuple=⟨…⟩` and `imasm derive word=…` and tells
operators never to hand-pick glyphs. Neither existed anywhere — kernel or tool.
An agent that followed the instruction hit nothing and reached for a
conventional method instead, so what looked like agents ignoring grammar-first
was the rule having no implementation to reach.

imasm(tuple=…) writes a tuple to its word. imasm(word=…, derive=true) reads a
word back to its tuple with its crystal address. The instruments still run on a
word, batched into the same boot.

Both directions are the kernel's, so a tuple an agent gets is the tuple the
kernel computes, and nothing is guessed.
