Local model resolution: paths, grammaformer, .modelz

Model resolution accepted "local" and "local:<name>" and nothing else, so a
model given as a filesystem path, a ~-relative path, a .modelz bundle or by the
name grammaformer fell through to the remote provider chain and failed there
rather than loading. All four now resolve locally, in odot_operator's argument
handling and in base_agent's constructor, which had the same test written twice
and needed both.
