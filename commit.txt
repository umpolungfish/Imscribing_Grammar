Read the criticality order from the table that owns it

`imscribe join loki_laufeyjarson odinn_allfather` raised KeyError on 𐑻. The Φ
absorption in the algebra carried its own ordinal map, written out by hand, and
that map held three of the five criticality values: 𐑢, ⊙, 𐑣. Any tuple carrying
𐑮 or 𐑻 crashed the meet and the join. Loki sits at 𐑻 and Óðinn at 𐑮, so the pair
missed the map on both sides.

The two absent values are the two that are not at integer ranks. Complex
criticality and the exceptional point sit between the real critical point and the
supercritical phase, at 2.33 and 2.67, and the hand-written map had integer
indices into a three-element list, so it could not have held them even with the
entries present. It reads from ORDINALS in canonical_primitives now, which is the
table the rest of the system already treats as the source, and it picks the lower
or higher of the pair by comparing ranks rather than indexing a list by one.

The same function was deciding which operation it was by looking for ∩, the set
intersection, in the operator symbol. The callers pass ⊓ and ⊔, the square cap and
cup, so the test never matched and every call took the join branch: a meet of two
criticalities returned the higher of them, and a configurable absorption rule
scoped to meet could never fire while one scoped to join fired during a meet.
Neither showed up as an error. It tests for the symbols the callers actually pass.
The pair above now meets at 𐑮 and joins at 𐑻, which is the way round it should be.

𐑧 is a sixth criticality value, carried by the transactinides out of
elem2imasm.py, and the canonical order does not rank it: the enum's own docstring
says the ordered set is five and lists it separately. So neither the lower nor the
higher of a pair means anything when 𐑧 is one of them, and rather than invent a
rank to put it at one end of an axis it is not on, that case now emits CONFLICT
and names itself in the conflicts list, which is what the algebra already does for
a categorical mismatch. All twenty-one pairs of criticality values resolve now,
where anything outside the three-value map used to be a crash.

The module docstring had Φ in the list of categorical primitives requiring exact
match. It is ordered, with an absorbing element, and it now says so, with the
order and its ranks written next to the other five.
