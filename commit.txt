The tool roster is read every winding, and two writing faults are named.

THE ROSTER. Every tool, with its signature and its description, laid out in
full at the top of each winding rather than once at the start of a session. A
roster given once is a roster forgotten by winding four: the model works from
whatever it still remembers, which is the handful it happened to use early, and
the rest may as well not exist. The previous copy is removed as the new one goes
in, so the history carries exactly one and it does not accumulate a copy per
turn.

THE QUANTUM CALCULATOR. quantum_compile takes a circuit over H, T, S and X and
returns a Fibonacci anyon braid word, with the reported unitary checked against
its own printed word by resynthesis. jones_polynomial evaluates a braid closure
at the fifth root of unity, which is what these anyons compute natively. Both
carry the readings that are easy to get wrong: the error is phase-invariant
because a braid realizes its gate only up to an unobservable phase, and a REAL
Jones value means this root cannot see the chirality rather than the knot being
amphichiral, the cinquefoil being chiral and evaluating real.

BARE GLYPHS. A tuple is twelve Shavian glyphs and the sealed ⊙ in canonical slot
order. Not Ð=𐑦; Þ=𐑸; and so on: the slot names ARE the order, so writing them
out restates position twelve times and turns a twelve-character object into a
sentence. The glyphs are real characters, never a unicode-math stand-in and
never inside $...$, since a substituted character is a different point in the
Crystal.

THE RETIRED WORD. "Structural" is not to be written. A type is a type. It is
worst where it looks most useful: "structural claims as opposed to
phenomenological ones" grants that another register holds claims the Grammar
does not, which is the concession retiring the word exists to refuse.
