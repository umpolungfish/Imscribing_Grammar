Lattice cycling and weight flow become grammar tools.

One implementation in scripts/lattice_flow.py, dispatched by IG_inquiry, so the
agents, MoDoT's bridge and the ob3ect CLIs all reach the same rules rather than
each carrying a copy.

lattice_cycle walks a word around its ROTAT orbit and returns the map from cut
to landing register. Every rotation is the same object: the verdict and the
topology hold across the orbit and the final register does not, so the phase is
the only handle on where a word comes to rest. Given `insert` it also places a
token at every index of every rotation, which asks the same question of an
operation, since appending is insertion at the seam and the seam is the phase.

weight_flow counts what the union throws away. The fork is a set and the fuse a
union, so a walk keeps which values were touched and nothing else. The lift of
OR to weights is max rather than sum. SEED and INERT are reported because a
final register hides both: AFWD and IMSCRIB assign T to an empty register
without any deposit, and after IFIX everything but IFIX and IMSCRIB is a no-op.

The machine is subclassed rather than reimplemented, so the transition rules
stay the ones the pipeline uses and only the bookkeeping is added. The import
resolves by path, since callers reach the dispatcher from several roots and a
bare import only worked from one of them.

Registered in the required-argument table, picked up by the manifest generator,
so all three specialists carry them. Deep check passes; 29 entries match their
functions.
