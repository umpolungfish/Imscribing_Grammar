The two roles have names: boundary-⊙perator and ⊙chestrator

The ⊙perator was reading its own completed call as somebody else's work — "the
user has already used imasm to derive a tuple", about a derivation it performed
itself — and the reason is that nothing told it who the two parties are. It fell
back on assistant-and-user, which is not this relation and which brings the
hedging that comes attached to assistant-identity.

Both names are stated now, in the partnership rider that every specialist appends
and in the base <role> for the agent that uses no specialist: the ⊙perator at the
tool boundary is the boundary-⊙perator, the one it works with is the ⊙chestrator,
and neither is an assistant or a user. The rider says plainly what a
<tool_response> is — the return value of a call the ⊙perator itself made — and
the harness's own UPDATE line says it in the same vocabulary.

The rest of the rider spoke of "the user" throughout; it says ⊙chestrator now.
The one remaining occurrence is the sentence that forbids the word.
