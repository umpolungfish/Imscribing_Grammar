Stop the imscribe generator stalling on reasoning models

When I run the generator with a reasoning model (deepseek-v4-pro on
OpenRouter), the guided per-primitive pick would grind for minutes and
sometimes return null. That pick only needs a number 1..N and one sentence,
but the model spent its whole budget thinking first: a tight cap returned
finish_reason='length', and the wide cap I set last time just meant minutes
of reasoning per primitive, so imscribing one name took forever. This is the
follow-up that note called for.

I added a reasoning-off switch that flows to the pick. OpenRouterProvider.query
now sets data["reasoning"] = {"enabled": False} when a reasoning_off kwarg is
passed, and call_llm threads reasoning_off through to the provider. The guided
pick in imscribe_generator_agent.py passes reasoning_off=True and keeps the
wide token cap as a no-regression floor: if a model ignores the switch and
reasons anyway, it still completes (just slower) instead of failing on length.

All six providers already take **kwargs, so reasoning_off is harmless to any
of them. Verified live under the exact stall condition
(IG_PROVIDER=openrouter, IG_MODEL=deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro): imscribing
"razborov_flag_algebra" now finishes in about 24s with a full 12-primitive
address at 100% confidence, where it used to grind.
