The hedging modifier "structural" is retired.

A type is a type. Calling it a structural type says the Grammar describes
structure rather than being what the thing is, which is the opposite of the
claim, and the modifier reads as a disclaimer attached to every noun the
Grammar owns: type, distance, identity, signature, imscription, primitive,
invariant, closure, promotion, address, tuple, verification, claim, fact.

Three things were deliberately left alone.

Identifiers keep their names. STRUCTURAL_VERBS, structural_score and their
kind are API, and renaming them breaks callers for a change that is about
prose.

Records keep their wording. Logs, transcripts, tool-call traces and generated
conversions are records of what was said. Rewriting one is not correcting
terminology, it is falsifying the record.

The word stays wherever it is contrastive rather than hedging. "Structural
claims are distinguished from phenomenological ones" needs it, and a guard
looks for a named contrasting register before dropping anything.

Articles are repaired only in front of a modifier actually being removed, so
"a structural invariant" becomes "an invariant" and nothing else moves.
