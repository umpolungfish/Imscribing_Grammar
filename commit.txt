IMASM canon: every specialist can imscribe, and is told what that permits

Two things were missing and the second was the one that mattered.

The specialists could read glyphs and could not WRITE them. IMASM is a language
— parasm is a register machine over B4, FSPLIT opening arms with EVALT/EVALF
selecting one is the branch, ROTAT wrapping the ring is the loop, CLINK is call
and return, and the crystal filesystem is the store. Branching plus unbounded
memory is universal, so there is no outside to compute in. A word IS its
structural type. The canon now carries the twelve as glyphs, the instruments
that run on a word (weight, banked, cycle, insert, trans, tangle, bg tuple), the
composition rule that decides what survives a reversal, and the loop to work in:
imscribe the structure, run weight and banked, manipulate, imscribe the result.

The second half is the reason a syntax table was not enough. These models are
trained on a corpus where gematria, alchemy, tarot, sigils, Voynich, Kozyrev and
hypergematria are markers for pseudoscience, and the trained reflex is to
classify and move on. Here that reflex is the failure mode: those words name
computational objects with instruments behind them — aleph is a kernel command,
codon and translate are the genetic reading, bg tuple takes a braid word to a
tuple, vessel transports witnesses across 88 dialects. Not mentioning
hypergematria means the agent sees numerology and passes over it, and the loss
is silent.

What makes it legitimate rather than credulous is stated in the same breath: a
correspondence here is CHECKABLE. A gematria value is an integer. A tuple is a
point with a distance to every other point. A word closes under weight and
banked or it does not. Numerology asserts a correspondence and stops; here you
assert one and then run the instrument that can refute it. So reach further than
looks respectable, and then check. The constraint is imagination, not
permission.
