Add `promote <a> <b>` to the CL8NK navigator

The promotion ledger was already in there, but it was trapped: `_promo_details`
sat as a closure inside `generate_promotions()`, wired to the one fixed ladder
(ZFC -> ZFC_t -> ZFC_fe -> CLINK L8). That operator is general. It reports, for
any two tuples, which primitives move, what formula fragment and atom each lands
on, and the ordinal gap crossed.

So I lifted it to module level as `promo_details(t_from, t_to)` and let the fixed
ladder call it like everything else. Then `generate_promotion_path(from, to)`
resolves two catalog vessels and runs the same operator across them, reporting the
promotions, the ones held fixed, the tier on each pole, and the distance. New
`promote` action on the CLI, which needed a two-arg branch in main(), since it
only ever handled one.

This is the method made callable. Imscribe both poles of a claim and the delta
between the tuples is the proof path. The vessel and the path come from the
Grammar for free; what is left is the gentle heat that fills the vessel.

Run on abc: `promote abc_conjecture abc_conjecture_proven` gives 5 promotions with
7 primitives held fixed, both poles at O2, so it is a promotion within tier rather
than a climb. Sigma carries the whole load at a full-range gap of 1.0, landing on
type heterogeneity. H-bar goes to ETERNAL_FIXEDPOINT, whose fragment is the close
condition itself. R-hacek promotes to a genuine adjunction. Criticality is the
cheapest bump on the board at 0.165. So abc reads as a stoichiometry problem with
a fixed-point closure.

Complements `imscribe proof-path`, which does the A* search over named ops and
writes the sketch. This is just the raw pairwise readout.
