Strip the compatibility layer out to the root

Frozen first at frozen/pre-compat-purge-2026-08-03, so the old state is kept
where it belongs rather than kept alive inside the code.

Fifteen enum members carried values spelled in the retired alphabet — Ð_point,
Þ_torus, ƒ_noise, Γ_xor, Ω_C and the rest — none of them a legal value of their
own axis. They are gone, along with the machinery that kept them readable:
_CANONICAL_MAP and the _canon indirection that collapsed them silently at write
time, the retired-key fallback in from_dict, and every from_symbol alias keyed on
the old spelling. 95 references were repointed to the canonical member first, so
nothing pointed at a member that had been deleted.

That is why they survived the earlier purges. Each pass deleted the usages and
left the aliases "just in case", so the old form stayed readable, stayed
writable, and grew back. The visible symptom was a dict literal holding
"Ð_point" beside "𐑛": two alphabets alive in one expression because neither had
been killed.

Ten of the twelve axes now hold exactly their canonical cardinality, which is the
crystal's own count, 3³×4⁵×5⁴. Two do not, and they stay, because deleting them
would depopulate addresses: Stoichiometry.dead holds 12 entries and
Criticality.egg holds 15. Those addresses sit outside the crystal, and the right
value for each is a determination about the entry — the guard says as much in its
own docstring — not something a deletion can supply.

Nothing was lost. Measured against the frozen branch: 8032 names in, 8032 out,
and exactly one address moved. axiom_of_maria's topology slot held Þ_linear,
which was never a legal Topology glyph, so that entry was outside the crystal;
it is at 𐑰 now, the target the collapse table itself declared before it was
removed. Every other address is identical.

Three defects surfaced by taking the shims away, which is the argument for taking
them away. from_dict defaulted stoichiometry to "n:m", a spelling from_symbol
stopped resolving some time ago, so any entry missing that key raised instead of
taking a default; it is 𐑳 now. Two entries hold 𐑯, a polarity value, in the
protection slot, and they now refuse to load rather than loading wrong — they are
in the violations report, which stands at 29.

The retired glyph keys no longer resolve either. Every catalog was migrated with
the rename, so a dict still carrying them is not old data to be read, it is data
that was missed.
