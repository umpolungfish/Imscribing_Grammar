Wrap the phase-match residual to the signed branch

phase_diff is normalized so its first element is exactly zero, so a good match
leaves the remaining residuals near zero. Taking the standard deviation over
[0, 2pi) then sends every residual that is zero-from-below to nearly a full
turn: a perfect match with float dust scores 2.91, worse than genuinely random
phases at 1.77. The metric inverts exactly where it is meant to be sharpest.

On the current inputs the change is small — the demo's headline moves from
1.818977 to 1.778632, and two of four configurations move by under 0.003 —
because the PE/QFT residuals are spread around the circle rather than
concentrated near the wrap. No conclusion drawn from these numbers changes. The
fix matters if the alignment ever gets good.
