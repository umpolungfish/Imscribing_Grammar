The twelve primitives take the IMASM glyphs

AS_ABOVE and SO_BELOW now write the primitives as the opcodes they are:

    ⊢ Dimensionality   ⊣ Topology         ≻ Relational      ≺ Polarity
    ═ Fidelity         ⊙ Kinetics         ∈ Granularity     ∋ Grammar
    ⁺ Criticality      × Chirality        ⊞ Stoichiometry   ¬ Protection

The old marks carried too much of their own meaning. Ð and Þ and ƒ and Ħ arrive
already saying something, and what they say is not what the axis says; the reader
brings a letter's history to a slot that only wants a position. The opcodes say
nothing on their own, which is the whole of their qualification, and the mapping
is one to one because the twelve axes and the twelve opcodes were always the same
twelve. The serpent eats its tail.

Every replacement is scoped to the primitive font, so nothing outside it moved:
SO_BELOW's 117 \Phi, 38 \Omega and 5 \Sigma are ordinary mathematics and are
untouched, as are the two places where AS_ABOVE talks about ⊙ as the alchemical
sign of the Stone rather than as a primitive. 475 marks changed in AS_ABOVE, 629
in SO_BELOW, and no old glyph survives anywhere in either file.

Two things had to be got right rather than swept. The manuscripts spelled two
different primitives with the same mark: AS_ABOVE wrote Polarity as ⊙ and
Criticality as \odot, which render identically, so a character-level pass would
have collapsed them. They are separated by their markup, and each went to its own
new glyph. And the critical point is a VALUE of Criticality as well as being the
axis, spelled with the same mark in both files and behind a macro in SO_BELOW.
Both went to ⁺, which keeps the identity that was already there: the axis and its
own self-referential value are the same mark, as they were when both were ⊙. ⊙ is
free now and belongs to Kinetics alone.

Everson Mono carries all twelve of the new glyphs. Both documents compile at the
page counts they had, 45 and 82, with no missing character that was not already
missing: SO_BELOW's nine are a ✓ it has never had.
