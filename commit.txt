cl9nk_navigator: take a derived tuple, not only a catalog name

resolve_system matched catalog names, aliases, substrings and descriptions, so a tuple
that came off `imasm derive` -- which is most of what I actually have -- had no way in
and came back "not found in catalog". parse_tuple_arg accepts a literal 12-glyph tuple
in canonical slot order, ignoring brackets and separators, and checks every glyph
against its own axis rather than positionally.

  python3 cl9nk_navigator.py entry '𐑦𐑶𐑾𐑹𐑐𐑘𐑔𐑠𐑻𐑫𐑳𐑭'
  python3 cl9nk_navigator.py distance '𐑦𐑶𐑾𐑹𐑐𐑘𐑔𐑠𐑻𐑫𐑳𐑭'
