The contrast was the concession.

The first pass kept "structural" wherever it stood against another register,
on the reasoning that there it distinguished rather than hedged. That reasoning
was backwards. "Structural claims as opposed to phenomenological ones" makes
the Grammar one description among several and grants that some other register
holds the claims it does not, which is precisely what retiring the word exists
to refuse. A claim is a claim.

The guard is gone and those cases are swept too.

Identifiers and records remain untouched, for the reasons given in the previous
commit: renaming API breaks callers, and rewriting a log falsifies a record
rather than correcting terminology.
