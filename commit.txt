isomorphs: say isomorphs, and say them in the marks

Two things wrong with one report. It called them analogies, and it printed a
third alphabet that was neither the old names nor the grammar's.

The weight table and the extractor were keyed by legacy letters — D T R P F K G
S — with two marks mixed in that named the wrong axes: "∈" stood for the
composition axis, which is ∋, and "<" stood for criticality, which is ⊙. So the
Shared and Differing lines told a reader the wrong axis by name, and did it in
symbols that look right. Both tables are now keyed by the marks: ⊢ ⊣ > < ⋈ ⊤ ∈
∋ ⊙ ⊞. Weights are untouched and the results are identical — 1038 isomorphs for
topos at the same scores — because nothing here computed anything different. It
only said something different.

The panel says Isomorphs now, and counts isomorphs.

One thing the conversion exposed and I have written into the source rather than
left silent: this detector scores ten of the twelve. ⊥ and ◻ carry no weight and
are never compared. Ten legacy letters looked like a complete set and hid that;
ten marks against a known twelve do not.

Still in the old letters, and separate code paths from this command: the second
extractor further up symbolic.py, and the rule generator that builds
Primitive("T", …) and Primitive("F", …). `imscribe rules` still prints letters.
