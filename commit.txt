⊢ Dimensionality: every underscored name gone

The first axis of the per-primitive purge. Ð_wynn, Ð_turnthree, Ð_invomega,
Ð_omega and the rest resolve to their Shavian values; the LaTeX subscript forms
Ð_{ß} Ð_{C} Ð_{;} Ð_{\omega} with them.

Three kinds of hit, treated differently. Real values became the value glyph.
Ordinary mathematics that an earlier sweep had glyph-substituted went back to
ASCII: $A_n$, $B_n$, $C_n$, $Ð_n$ in E8G2.tex is the classical Lie series D_n,
Ð_p in the SIC-POVM paper is the displacement operator D_p, and Ð_H / Ð_A are
hydrogen-bond distances. And English words whose D had been eaten were restored:
HYBRIÐ_SUPRA_TEMP was HYBRID, BROAÐ_SEQ was BROAD.

Also gone: the eight spent migration scripts under .stuff/scripts, the subscript
fallback in resolve_ordinal_key (which is how a value that had stopped being
legal went on resolving to something anyway), and the whole PRIM_LATEX table in
IG_latex.py, whose only job was rendering a value as a retired glyph with a
subscript.

Zero remain anywhere except 2m3iosis/crystal_fs/*.jsonl, which are kernel run
logs rather than code or data. Eleven axes to go.
