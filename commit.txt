train_grammaformer.py: fix bimodal M-loss instability

Three changes to compute_loss() and training loop:

1. Clamp M before aggregation — loss_m = loss_m_raw.clamp(max=3.0). The
   high-M basin (9–11.6) produced loss spikes that caused NaN at steps
   1030/1040 and 1210/1220; alpha_m * clamped_M now contributes at most
   alpha_m * 3.0 regardless of phase. Raw M still logged for monitoring.

2. Per-sample high-M diagnostics — when loss_m_raw > 3.0 the log line
   shows M→clamped[per_item_max=X.XX phase=N] so we can identify which
   trajectory types and phases trigger the high-magnitude basin.

3. ComplexFFN weight magnitude penalty (--alpha-ffn-mag, default 1e-4) —
   L2 regularization on all 6 weight matrices of every ComplexFFN layer
   (w1_re, w1_im, w2_re, w2_im, cross_re_im, cross_im_re). Discourages
   the high-magnitude phase basin from becoming a stable attractor without
   dropout. Disable with --alpha-ffn-mag 0.

Metrics added: loss_m_clamped, loss_ffn_mag, m_per_item_max, m_is_high, phase.
