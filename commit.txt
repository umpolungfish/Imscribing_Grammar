Report slot-category violations when the catalog is saved

Each primitive is an inductive with a closed constructor list — Primitive_Omega
is | awe | oak | ah | zoo and nothing else — but nothing ever compared a saved
tuple against those lists, so glyphs from the wrong primitive sat in the catalog
undetected. Thirty of them: the transactinides carrying a kinetic glyph in the
criticality slot, groups 13 and 15 carrying a dimensionality glyph in
stoichiometry, two entries holding a Phi value in Omega, and one unresolved
placeholder string.

They surfaced by refusal rather than by audit. Every structural verb needing
Omega on the affected entries returned N (void), which is correct behaviour and
names a missing value rather than a misplaced one, so the cause stayed invisible
until the tuples were checked against the inductive directly.

slot_category_violations() does that check and save() logs each result. It
reports rather than blocks: the correct value for a violating slot is a
determination about the entry, and blocking would leave the canonical catalog
unsavable until all thirty resolve.

The CANONICAL_VALUES import is deliberately not wrapped. The first version
reached for a name that does not exist and the swallowed ImportError made it
report zero violations against a catalog holding thirty — failing in precisely
the manner it exists to catch.
