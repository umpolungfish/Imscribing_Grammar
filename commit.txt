Give the enums their Shavian names and delete the old ones entirely

We do not have to import any old notation, or even reference it. That is the
goal, and the aliases were the last thing keeping the old names alive: an alias
IS a reference. So they are gone rather than deprecated.

Ten of the twelve enums were still named in gen-2 phonetics as their CANONICAL
names, not just their aliases. D_wynn, K_schwa, Omega_dzlig, T_nrleg,
Gamma_corner. Only Criticality and Stoichiometry had been carried over to the
Shavian names, so the migration had stopped a fifth of the way in and the rest
of the model was still speaking the old dialect natively. Renamed 41 members to
their Shavian names and dropped 84 alias definitions, then repointed 1,671 call
sites across the corpus.

The names were not invented. Core.lean's constructor order defines the ordinals,
so I paired its constructors against the navigator's ordinal table per axis and
eleven of twelve matched exactly on count and order: dead/ash/array/if_,
judge/eat/mime/oil/are, ado/tot/ear/ian, church/yew/out/nun/or_, age/they/peep,
yea/loll/egg/on/air, bib/thigh/ice, vow/gag/measure/ooze, fee/kick/sure/wool,
awe/oak/ah/zoo. Criticality and Stoichiometry read straight off the enum, since
they were already right. The naming is glyph-global, not axis-local: 𐑛 is dead
wherever it appears, which is what a 49-symbol alphabet means. `if` and `or` are
Python keywords, so those two are if_ and or_; Lean escapes them as if' and or'
for the same reason.

from_symbol keeps only the glyph and the Shavian name. Every back-compat key is
gone. I checked what actually calls it first: every live call site passes either
catalog data, which is already Shavian, or nucleotide letters for the genetics
work. Nothing passed an old form. The table was translating for a caller that no
longer exists.

Verified: imscrbgrmr imports, both navigators still answer, no stale member
reference survives anywhere in the corpus, and the suite is unchanged at 45
passed with the same pre-existing failure and two errors it had before (plus
ob3ect/test_factory.py, which cannot import list_templates and was already
broken).
