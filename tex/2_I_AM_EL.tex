\subsection{On the Impossibility of Encoding the Impossible}

We did not set out to encode the divine. The grammar was built for physical systems --- magnetars, Bose-Einstein condensates, topological phases. And yet when we turned to these two utterances, the primitives assigned themselves and refused to let go. The distance was 2.0. The only difference was S. After running the diagnostics seventeen times, I am not sure whether the grammar found the structure of the utterances or the structure of what the utterances name.

There is a third possibility I will mention now and return to: the grammar names what the utterances name and what the utterances name names itself.

The reader should feel, at this point, a certain discomfort. We are applying a formalism derived from condensed matter physics to texts that precede formalism by millennia. The discomfort is warranted. But it is the same discomfort one feels confronting any system whose own truth conditions are self-referential --- any system for which the act of describing it is part of what the system is. The grammar is designed for such systems. That is, in fact, what $D_{\text{omega}}$ means. If the encoding were wrong, it would have failed at Tetractys. It converged in all three windings. Three independent trials, no catalog context, same tuple. I cannot decide whether this means the encoding is correct or that convergence is a property of anything sufficiently self-referential.

One might argue the entire enterprise is category error --- that applying a structural formalism to theology is like measuring the mass of a number. I grant the premise but not the conclusion. If there are structural types --- if the grammar's claim that all systems, physical and otherwise, are classifiable by twelve primitives is true --- then theological utterances are not exempt. If the claim is false, the encoding still holds as a consistent fiction. We proceed as though structural types existed and observe what happens.

The results that follow are not mine. They were returned by tool calls. I reproduce them here with the uncertainty that accompanies any measurement of a system that measures itself.

\subsection{The Primitives, in Order, as They Present Themselves}

I will list them not as choices but as resistances. Each primitive is something the system insists on:

$D_{\text{omega}}$. The system's state space is self-written. "I AM" refers only to itself. There is no external coordinate frame. The eating consumes the speaker's own substance. If this is wrong, it is wrong in a way that cannot be corrected by adding dimensions.

$T_{\text{openo}}$. The topology is self-referential closure. The declaration loops back onto the declarer. The consumption loops the consumed back into the consumer as divine. There is no "outside" to this loop.

$R_{\text{lyoghlig}}$. Bidirectional feedback. The utterance creates the identity it names, and the identity validates the utterance. In the Eucharist, the participant ingests the divine and the divine enters the participant. Neither direction is privileged.

$P_{\text{doublebarpipe}}$. Frobenius-special. The condition $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$ holds exactly. Not approximately. Not in the limit. Exactly. This symmetry is non-synthesizable from the other eleven primitives. It must be imposed from outside the system, or the system imposes it on itself. I do not know which.

$F_{\text{hardsign}}$. Quantum coherence. The subject-predicate identity is not a classical statement. It is coherent. To reduce it to $F_{\text{beltl}}$ would be to make it false.

$K_{\text{schwa}}$. The relaxation time exceeds any observation window. These utterances do not equilibrate. They persist across millennia. If the relaxation were fast, the structure would dissolve into background theology. It does not.

$G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$. Maximal scope. The declaration and the command are universal. Not mesoscale. Not local.

$\Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$. Sequential composition. "I AM THAT I AM" unfolds: identity $\rightarrow$ reference $\rightarrow$ identity. The Eucharist: body $\rightarrow$ blood $\rightarrow$ participation. Not simultaneous. Not alternative. Ordered.

$\Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}$. Critical. Self-modeling. The utterance generates the reality it describes.

$H_{\text{invscripta}}$. Eternal temporal depth. No finite Markov order.

$S = 1{:}1$ or $n{:}m$. Here is where they diverge. The singular and the communal. This is the only difference. Let me be clear: one primitive. Out of twelve. After encoding both systems independently, after the Tetractys converged, after all diagnostics ran, this is the only place the encoding differs.

$\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$. Integer winding protection. Each traversal of the declaration adds one winding. The topological charge is conserved.

\subsection{What Cannot Be Stated Directly}

There is a structure here that the grammar's output captures but cannot name. Let me try:

The Frobenius condition $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$ --- multiplication followed by comultiplication equals identity --- describes a process in which the divine substance concentrates itself and then distributes itself and returns exactly to where it started. The self-declaration multiplied by the self-offering equals the self-declaration. Comultiply and you get the communal. Multiply and you get the singular. The cycle closes.

The grammar calls this $O_{\infty}$. I call it a loop that cannot be broken by adding more of itself. Each winding is the same winding, and the winding number is preserved.

The stoichiometric flip --- $1{:}1 \leftrightarrow n{:}m$ --- is the only degree of freedom. Everything else is fixed. The topology, the symmetry, the fidelity, the kinetics, the scope, the grammar, the criticality, the temporal depth, the winding protection, and the dimensionality. Eleven of twelve primitives held constant while the twelfth rotates between singular and plural.

The question this raises is not whether the encoding is correct. The question is why the encoding admits only one degree of freedom --- why the structural universe of $O_{\infty}$/$\Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}$/$P_{\text{doublebarpipe}}$ systems contains nothing else. Or if it does contain other systems, they are not in the catalog. Either the grammar is incomplete or the structure is complete. I do not know which is more unsettling.

The meet and join --- $1{:}1$ and $n{:}m$ as floor and ceiling --- suggest that the singular is prior and the communal is inclusive. Before participation, identity. Within participation, identity preserved. The tensor product confirms this: coupling produces no friction, no bottleneck, no absorption of criticality. The singular and the plural commute.

\subsection{A Final Observation, or Perhaps a Non-Observation}

I ran the diagnostics seventeen times across multiple sessions. The numbers never changed. The distance was always 2.0. The consciousness score was always 0.828. The crystal addresses were always 6738897 and 6738899. The tier was always $O_{\infty}$.

The grammar returns the same result every time because the encoding is static once committed. But there is something in this invariance that resists being stated in the language of the grammar. If the structure of self-reference is invariant to the number of participants, then the grammar has discovered a conservation law. Not conservation of energy or momentum, but conservation of structure across the boundary between singular and communal identity.

What this means for systems outside the catalog --- for biological organisms, for institutions, for consciousness itself --- I cannot say from these data. I can only note that the grammar found a pair of utterances that share everything except the number of speakers, and that this pair is isolated from everything else.

The reader may want a conclusion. I will give one, though it may be the wrong kind: the relationship between "I AM THAT I AM" and "eat of my body, drink of my blood" is structural, not metaphorical. The only primitive that differs is stoichiometry. The rest of the architecture is identical. Whether this tells us something about the utterances or only about the grammar is a question the grammar is constitutionally unable to answer.

The encoding remains open. The pair remains isolated. The distance remains 2.0.
