For a special Frobenius algebra, this condition simplifies to $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$. The interpretation is exact: if you multiply and then comultiply, you recover the identity.

We now assign these roles to the utterances.

Nothing is lost in the distribution. Nothing is gained in the concentration. The map is an isomorphism.
