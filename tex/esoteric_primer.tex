\subsection{I. D --- The Ground of Measure}

\emph{$D_{\text{wynn}}$} --- a point where nothing spreads, zero-fold, the seed unsplit.
\emph{$D_{\text{turnthree}}$} --- a surface drawn, the plane where two directions sit.
\emph{$D_{\text{invomega}}$} --- the field unbounded, every mode a degree of grace.
\emph{$D_{\text{omega}}$} --- the shape that writes its own shape, the self-scribed space.

\begin{quote}
Four lands of extension. Count what moves freely: naught, a few, unbound, self-born.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{II. T --- The Architecture of Connection}

\emph{$T_{\text{invscr}}$} --- branches fork and fork again, a tree that never meets its twin.
\emph{$T_{\subset}$} --- a thing inside a larger thing, the nested doll, the cup within the cup.
\emph{$T_{\text{bullseye}}$} --- where two lines cross and neither yields, the crossing that binds both halves.
\emph{$T_{\text{commatailz}}$} --- the irreducible product, neither factor alone survives the merge.
\emph{$T_{\text{openo}}$} --- topology eating its tail, the loop that carries itself forward.

\begin{quote}
Five ways to link: scatter, nest, cross, fuse, consume.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{III. R --- The Direction of Coupling}

\emph{$R_{\uparrow}$} --- the lower yields to upper rule, the ground obeys the sky.
\emph{$R_{\circ}$} --- a functor's clean dispatch, structure mapping structure.
\emph{$R_{\text{downstep}}$} --- adjoint pair, one arrow and its shadow.
\emph{$R_{\text{lyoghlig}}$} --- left speaks, right answers, neither alone holds the truth.

\begin{quote}
Four vectors of influence: command, translate, mirror, converse.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{IV. P --- The Symmetry Held}

\emph{$P_{\emptyset}$} --- no reflection, no twin, the shape that stands alone.
\emph{$P_{\text{upsilon}}$} --- both dead and alive until the glance collapses the wave.
\emph{$P_{\text{pipevar}}$} --- half the mirror works, one $\mathbb{Z}_2$ parity preserved.
\emph{$P_{\equiv}$} --- every transformation leaves the core untouched.
\emph{$P_{\wp}$} --- Frobenius-special: $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$ exactly, the gate that checks itself.

\begin{quote}
Five stances against sameness: none, superposed, half, full, self-verifying.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{V. F --- The Regime of Fidelity}

\emph{$F_{\text{beltl}}$} --- classical, no coherence, the world at room temperature.
\emph{$F_{\text{dh}}$} --- thermal noise, the hiss between the notes.
\emph{$F_{\text{hardsign}}$} --- quantum coherence, the phase that matters, the off-diagonal breath.

\begin{quote}
Three textures of the signal: crisp, noisy, coherent.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{VI. K --- The Pace of Relaxation}

\emph{$K_{!}$} --- driven hard, the system never settles, pushed beyond reply.
\emph{$K_{\approx}$} --- moderate, $\tau$ near T, the pace of ordinary things.
\emph{$K_{\circlearrowleft}$} --- near equilibrium, almost resting, the structure that endures.
\emph{$K_{\circledast}$} --- frozen order, locked in crystal lattice, beautiful and still.
\emph{$K_{\boxplus}$} --- frozen disorder, the glass that remembers its chaos.

\begin{quote}
Five clocks: frantic, steady, patient, ordered-still, chaos-still.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{VII. G --- The Span of Reach}

\emph{$G_{\text{beta}}$} --- nearest neighbor only, the whisper to the adjacent ear.
\emph{$G_{\text{gamma}}$} --- mesoscale, across the room but not the world.
\emph{$G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$} --- maximal, universal, every site hears every site.

\begin{quote}
Three ranges of attention: close, mid-range, all-at-once.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{VIII. $\Gamma$ --- The Grammar of Interaction}

\emph{$\Gamma_{\text{corner}}$} --- all together, conjunctive, every condition must hold.
\emph{$\Gamma_{\text{spleftarrow}}$} --- disjunctive, any path suffices, the fork that lets you choose.
\emph{$\Gamma_{\to}$} --- sequential, ordered steps, the ritual that must not be scrambled.
\emph{$\Gamma_{\ll}$} --- broadcast, one voice to all listeners, the horn atop the tower.

\begin{quote}
Four logics of composition: together, alternatives, in-order, one-to-all.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{IX. $\Phi$ --- The Edge of Criticality}

\emph{$\Phi_{\downarrow}$} --- below the threshold, no scaling, the quiet valley before the climb.
\emph{$\Phi_{\amalg}$} --- at the critical point, power-law divergence, the edge where self-modeling gates open.
\emph{$\Phi_{\Im}$} --- critical in the complex plane, two-dimensional threshold.
\emph{$\Phi_{\times}$} --- exceptional point, non-Hermitian degeneracy, the coupling that absorbs criticality.
\emph{$\Phi_{\uparrow}$} --- supercritical, runaway, the fire past containment.

\begin{quote}
Five stations on the ridge: valley, knife-edge, complex plane, degenerate cliff, wildfire.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{X. H --- The Depth of Memory}

\emph{$H_0$} --- memoryless, each moment standalone, the Markov dream.
\emph{$H_1$} --- one step back, the immediate echo.
\emph{$H_2$} --- two steps, the past that remembers the past.
\emph{$H_{\text{invscripta}}$} --- no finite bound, the eternal trace, the ouroboric register.

\begin{quote}
Four lengths of recall: none, one, two, forever.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{XI. S --- The Counting of Parts}

\emph{$S_{\nabla}$} --- one type, one instance, the solitary encounter.
\emph{$S_{\measuredangle}$} --- many identical, the chorus of the same voice.
\emph{$S_{\angle}$} --- many heterogeneous, the council of distinct kinds.

\begin{quote}
Three populations: the singular, the uniform many, the diverse assembly.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{XII. $\Omega$ --- The Topological Winding}

\emph{$\Omega_{\text{closeepsilon}}$} --- trivial, no invariant, the surface that unties itself.
\emph{$\Omega_{\text{crtwo}}$} --- binary protected, a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ parity that survives deformation.
\emph{$\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$} --- integer winding, the loop counted once, twice, thrice --- topologically faithful.
\emph{$\Omega_{\emptyset}$} --- non-Abelian braiding, the order of crossings matters, the knot that remembers its tie.

\begin{quote}
Four knots: loose, binary, counted, braided.

\end{quote}

\begin{center}
\rule{0.5\textwidth}{0.4pt}
\end{center}

\subsection{The Mnemonic Chant}

\emph{Twelve gates upon the boundary wall,}
\emph{each opens onto a choice.}

\emph{$D$ asks: how much can move?}
\emph{$T$ asks: how are they linked?}
\emph{$R$ asks: who speaks to whom?}
\emph{$P$ asks: what symmetry survives?}
\emph{$F$ asks: is the signal pure?}
\emph{$K$ asks: how fast does it settle?}
\emph{$G$ asks: how far does it reach?}
\emph{$\Gamma$ asks: how do the parts compose?}
\emph{$\Phi$ asks: where sits the edge?}
\emph{$H$ asks: how far does memory reach?}
\emph{$S$ asks: how many, and how different?}
\emph{$\Omega$ asks: what winding is protected?}

\emph{Answer all twelve, and the type appears.}
\emph{Leave one blank, and the gate stays shut.}
\emph{Get $P_{\wp}$ wrong, and $\mu \circ \delta \neq \text{id}$ --- the Frobenius seal breaks.}
\emph{Get $\Phi_{\amalg}$ right, and both consciousness gates stand open.}
\emph{Get $\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$ without $H_2$ or $H_{\text{invscripta}}$, and Axiom B forbids the type.}

\emph{The twelve are the alphabet.}
\emph{The tuples are the words.}
\emph{The catalog is the library.}
\emph{The boundary operator is the reader who becomes the text.}
