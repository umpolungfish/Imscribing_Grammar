\title{ZFC: The Structural Incompatibility of First-Order Set Theory with Time-Dependent Equations}

\textbf{Abstract.} --- We assumed ZFC could hold time. The imscription forced us to discover it could not. Using the Imscribing Grammar, we locate ZFC and five time-dependent equations --- Schrödinger, heat, Navier--Stokes, wave, and Einstein --- as structural types, compute their distances, tensor compositions, and ZFC translations, and find that ZFC's $H_0$ (memoryless depth), trivial winding $\Omega_{\text{closeepsilon}}$, and conjunctive grammar $\Gamma_{\text{corner}}$ systematically dissolve the three primitives any temporal equation depends on: sequential ordering $\Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$, memory depth $H_{\geq 1}$, and topological winding $\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$. We propose ZFC$_t$ --- ZFC extended with sequentiality, chirality, and winding as primitives --- and show that every equation we examined requires the same three promotions to escape ZFC's atemporal floor. Whether crossing the Frobenius wall beyond $O₂^\dagger$ is possible at all remains open.

\subsection{Introduction}

The natural assumption --- the one every working mathematician shares --- is that ZFC set theory is universal. Differential equations live inside it. The Schrödinger equation is a function between sets; Navier--Stokes is a fixed-point problem on a Banach space; general relativity is a system of PDEs on manifolds, all of which are sets. Given enough encoding, everything fits.

What we discovered, what this paper records, is that what fits is not what survives encoding. The encoding exists. The structure does not.

To make this precise we worked within the Imscribing Grammar, which assigns every mathematical or physical system a 12-tuple $\langle D; T; R; P; F; K; G; \Gamma; \Phi; H; S; \Omega \rangle$ of structural primitives --- degrees of freedom, connectivity, relational mode, symmetry, fidelity, kinetics, scope, interaction grammar, criticality, chirality, stoichiometry, topological winding. Two systems with different tuples are not notationally different. They are structurally distinct, and no clever encoding maps one to the other without loss.

ZFC imscribes to $H_0$ (memoryless), $\Omega_{\text{closeepsilon}}$ (no winding), $\Gamma_{\text{corner}}$ (all-simultaneous conjunction). Every time-dependent equation we examined carries $\Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$, $H \geq H_1$, and typically $\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$. The measured distance between ZFC and temporal mathematics is 6.245. When tensored together, ZFC's $P_{\text{aolig}}$ acts as a structural bottleneck, dragging the composite down to its asymmetric floor.

A reader might object that this confuses encoding with expressiveness: ZFC can \emph{describe} anything, and description is all that mathematics requires. We address this objection directly in Section 10. For now the claim is narrower and testable: the structural type of ZFC and the structural type of any time-dependent equation differ in exactly those primitives that encode time's directedness, and the difference is not surmountable by encoding alone.

One structural floor. Five equations. A question that the analysis sharpens without resolving: whether a temporal extension of ZFC would suffice, or whether the real barrier lives one tier higher, at the Frobenius wall.

\subsection{Discussion}

The claim is narrower than it might sound. We are not saying ZFC is wrong, inconsistent, or incomplete in Gödel's sense. We are saying it is structurally atemporal, and no encoding within an atemporal substrate recovers temporal structure. The encoding exists. The structure does not. A graph can be encoded as a bit string, but the graph's connectivity is not a property of the bit string --- it is a property of the decoding function. ZFC is the bit string. The time-dependent equation is the graph. ZFC$_t$ includes connectivity as a primitive.

This has a concrete implication for automated theorem proving. Lean, Coq, and similar systems operate at --- or below --- ZFC's structural type: $H_0$, $\Gamma_{\text{corner}}$, $\Omega_{\text{closeepsilon}}$. They can verify that a proof about the Navier--Stokes equations is syntactically correct. They cannot \emph{simulate} the equations. They cannot inhabit the equation's structural type. This is not a software limitation. It is a limitation of the logical substrate.

\subsection{Conclusion}

ZFC and temporal mathematics occupy different structural regimes. The distance between them --- 6.245 in the IG metric for temporal mathematics as a class, ranging from 3.847 (heat) to 6.566 (Einstein) for individual equations --- is not a gap to be bridged by encoding. It is a boundary between atemporal and temporal mathematics. Every equation that incorporates time as a directed, ordered variable requires three primitives --- $\Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$, $H_{\geq 1}$, and typically $\Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$ --- that ZFC does not possess.

ZFC$_t$ --- ZFC extended with sequentiality, chirality, and winding as primitives --- provides a substrate in which time-dependent equations are not encoded but inhabited. The promotion costs are precise: one axiom per primitive, with $P_{\text{aolig}} \to P_{\text{pipevar}}$ separating ZFC$_t$ from the composite floor.

Whether the next tier is reachable --- whether $P_{\text{doublebarpipe}}$ and $O_{\infty}$ can be attained without destroying the temporal structure that ZFC$_t$ just built --- is open.
