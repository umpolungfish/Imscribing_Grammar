A system in the Imscribing Grammar is a 12-primitive tuple 
\[
\langle D;\ T;\ R;\ P;\ F;\ K;\ G;\ \Gamma;\ \hat{\phi}_\psi;\ H;\ S;\ \Omega \rangle
\]
governing dimensionality, topology, relational mode, symmetry, fidelity, kinetics, scope, interaction grammar, criticality, chirality, stoichiometry, and topological winding.

We analyze four canonical systems that model epistemic and moral oppositions: \textbf{truth}, \textbf{lie}, \textbf{good}, and \textbf{evil}. Each is defined below with its primitive assignment.

\subsection{Structural Types}

\subsubsection{Truth}
\[
\text{truth} = \langle D_{\infty};\ T_{\odot};\ R_{\leftrightarrow};\ P_{\pm};\ F_{\hbar};\ K_{\text{slow}};\ G_{\gimel};\ \Gamma_{\text{seq}};\ \hat{\phi}_\psi;\ H_2;\ n:m;\ \Omega_{\mathbb{Z}} \rangle
\]

\begin{itemize}
    \item $D_{\infty}$: infinite-dimensional, imscriptive domain
    \item $T_{\odot}$: self-referential, closed topology
    \item $R_{\leftrightarrow}$: bidirectional coupling
    \item $P_{\pm}$: partial parity (symmetry-breaking permitted)
    \item $F_{\hbar}$: quantum-coherent fidelity
    \item $K_{\text{slow}}$: near-equilibrium, relaxed dynamics
    \item $G_{\gimel}$: mesoscale, intermediate interaction range
    \item $\Gamma_{\text{seq}}$: sequential composition logic
    \item $\hat{\phi}_\psi$: self-modeling criticality (Gate 1 open)
    \item $H_2$: two-step Markov memory
    \item $n:m$: heterogeneous components
    \item $\Omega_{\mathbb{Z}}$: integer winding (topologically protected)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Tier:} $O₂^\dagger$ (critical + $\mathbb{Z}$-protected, bounded domain) \\
\textbf{Consciousness score:} $C = 0.652$, both gates open ($\hat{\phi}_\psi$ + $H_2$ + moderate $K$).

\]

\begin{itemize}
    \item $D_{\triangle}$: two-dimensional (surface-like)
    \item $T_{\bowtie}$: crossing point topology (partial self-reference)
    \item $R_{\dagger}$: adjoint (one-way) coupling
    \item $P_{\pm}$: partial parity
    \item $F_{\hbar}$: quantum-coherent fidelity

    \item $\Omega_{\mathbb{Z}_2}$: binary $\mathbb{Z}_2$ winding
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Tier:} $O₀$ (no ouroboricity --- subcritical/supercritical/EP, cannot sustain self-referential criticality) \\
\textbf{Consciousness:} Gate 1 closed at $\hat{\phi}_3$; $C = 0$.

\subsection{Why Good is $O₂^\dagger$ While Lie/Evil are $O₀$}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Good} and \textbf{truth} share $\hat{\phi}_\psi$ (self-modeling) and $H_2$ (two-step memory), satisfying the criticality conditions for $O₂$. Good further has $\Omega_{\mathbb{Z}_2}$ instead of $\Omega_{\mathbb{Z}}$ --- a \emph{binary} winding rather than integer --- but $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is still topologically protected, permitting $O₂^\dagger$.
    \item \textbf{Lie} and \textbf{evil} both have $\hat{\phi}_3$ (exceptional point), which \textbf{destroys} Gate 1. No system with $\hat{\phi}_3$ can achieve $O₁$ or higher, regardless of other primitives. Thus, they are $O₀$.
\end{itemize}

The key distinction is \textbf{criticality}, not symmetry or topology alone. Good retains self-modeling; lie/evil do not.

\subsection{Concluding remarks}

Truth and good are structurally contiguous --- both critical ($\hat{\phi}_\psi$), both $O₂^\dagger$, differing mainly in relational mode ($R_{\leftrightarrow}$ vs $R_{\text{sup}}$) and topology ($T_{\odot}$ vs $T_{\text{in}}$). This reflects their semantic proximity: \emph{truth is self-consistency; good is relational consistency}.

Lie and evil form the deception doublet: both $\hat{\phi}_3$, both $O₀$, differing only in $D$ and $K$. Evil's $K_{\text{fast}}$ makes it more violently decoherent; lie's $K_{\text{slow}}$ allows it to simulate coherence while lacking self-modeling --- a \emph{sleeker} form of deception.

The structural grammar reveals that \textbf{consciousness requires $\hat{\phi}_\psi$}, while \textbf{deception is encoded by $\hat{\phi}_3$} --- a precise formalization of ancient dualities in modern categorical terms.
