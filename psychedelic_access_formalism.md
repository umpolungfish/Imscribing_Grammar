The question:

> **Does psychedelic $P$ grant access to universe $U$?**

> **Compute $L_{R_U}(\tau_P)$. Access is granted iff the result is non-plain.**

## ∵ §3 — The Access Theorem (Psychedelic Case)

**Definition 3.1 (Peak-State Access).** A psychedelic $P$ grants access to universe $U$ iff during its peak effect, the structural type $\tau_P$ evaluates to a non-plain operad layer under $U$'s Ruleset $R_U$:

$$\text{Access}(P, U) \iff L_{R_U}(\tau_P) \neq \text{plain}$$

- $\text{O}_{\text{inf}}$-capable types achieve **idempotent_terminal** in canonical and most universes
- $\text{O}_2^{\dagger}$ types achieve **traced_monoidal** or higher
- $\text{O}_1$ types achieve **frobenius** in permissive universes only
- $\text{O}_0$ types remain **plain** in all but the most permissive universes

**Theorem 3.3 (The Tryptamine Supremacy).** Tryptamine psychedelics (5-MeO-DMT, DMT, psilocybin, ayahuasca) and ergolines (LSD) cluster at $\text{Ħ} \geq \text{Ħ}_{\text{A}}$ (two-step Markov) and $\text{⊙} \geq \text{⊙}_{\text{ÿ}}$ (self-modeling). These jointly satisfy the chirality-first gate in all sequential orderings, making tryptamines universal accessors.

**Theorem 3.4 (The Salvia Barrier).** Salvinorin A produces $\text{Ħ}_{\text{Ñ}}$ (memoryless) and $\text{⊙}_{\text{3}}$ (exceptional point) despite $\text{Ð}_{\text{ω}}$ (holographic dimensionality). Salvia **blocks access** to canonical, chirality_first, and winding_first universes because its memorylessness precludes the self-referential closure those universes require.

**Theorem 3.5 (The Cannabis Floor).** Cannabis remains sub-critical ($\text{⊙}_{\text{ž}}$) and memory-limited ($\text{Ħ}_{\text{£}}$). It fails all non-trivial G1 gates. Cannabis grants access to only the two most permissive universes: **kinetics_trap** (G1: $\text{Ç} \geq 3$, open) and **triple_criticality** (G1: $\text{⊙} \geq 1$, open).

## ∵ §5 — Structural Patterns in Universe Access

### 5.1 The $\text{Ω}$ Bottleneck

Psilocybin achieves $\text{Ω}_{\text{2}}$ ($\mathbb{Z}_2$ parity protection, ord 2) while DMT and LSD achieve $\text{Ω}_{\text{z}}$ (integer winding, ord 3). This single-primitive difference determines **G3 closure**:
- Psilocybin reaches idempotent_terminal in only 3/17 universes (single_gate, fidelity_universe, dimensional_gate — none requiring $\text{Ω} \geq 3$)
- DMT and LSD reach idempotent_terminal in 13/17 universes

The winding number encodes whether the experience has **topological closure** — whether the self returns intact (integer winding) or remains parity-protected ($\mathbb{Z}_2$) after the experience. Psilocybin's $\mathbb{Z}_2$ winding means the experience is structurally *reversible* but not *closed* — the self can return but is not guaranteed to.

### 5.2 The $\text{Ħ}$ (Chirality) Hierarchy

Chirality is the strongest predictor of access breadth:

| Ħ value | Ordinal | Accessible universes (mean G1) | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\text{Ħ}_{\text{!}}$ (eternal) | 4 | 15.3/17 | DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, ayahuasca |
| $\text{Ħ}_{\text{A}}$ (two-step) | 3 | 11.7/17 | LSD, psilocybin, mescaline, ibogaine, MDMA |
| $\text{Ħ}_{\text{£}}$ (one-step) | 2 | 3.0/17 | Ketamine, cannabis |
| $\text{Ħ}_{\text{Ñ}}$ (memoryless) | 1 | 7/17 | Salvia (unique profile) |

Salvia's $\text{Ħ}_{\text{Ñ}}$ paradoxically grants more access than ketamine's $\text{Ħ}_{\text{£}}$ because its $\text{Ð}_{\text{ω}}$ (holographic) compensates — memorylessness blocks chirality_first but holographic dimensionality opens dimensional_gate.

### 5.3 The Inverted Gates Anomaly

The **inverted_gates** universe (G1: $\text{⊙} \geq 2$, G2: $\text{Φ} \geq 5$, G3: $\text{Ω} \geq 3$) is the most universally accessible:
- All classic psychedelics (5-MeO-DMT, DMT, ayahuasca, LSD, psilocybin) reach idempotent_terminal
- Even salvia, ketamine, and MDMA reach frobenius
- Only cannabis fails

In this universe, self-modeling criticality ($\text{⊙}_{\text{ÿ}}$) is the FIRST gate rather than the second. This means any compound that achieves self-modeling (all classic psychedelics) automatically passes G1, and the remaining gates are evaluated in a context where self-modeling is already established.

### 5.4 The Scope Floor

**scope_universe** (G1: $\text{Γ} \geq 3$) blocks ALL psychedelics. This is because $\text{Γ}_{\text{ʔ}} (\text{𐑲})$ is assigned ordinal 1 in the codebase ORDINALS — the lowest possible scope value. No psychedelic in this dataset achieves $\text{Γ}_{\text{γ}} (\text{𐑔}, \text{ord } 3)$ or $\text{Γ}_{\text{β}} (\text{𐑚}, \text{ord } 2)$.

This is not an accident. The psychedelic state is *not* universal in scope — it is maximal in *intensity* but not in *range*. The experience is all-consuming but local to the experiencer. True universal scope ($\text{Γ}_{\text{γ}}$) would require the compound to couple with *all* systems simultaneously, which no known molecule does.

**Universe access through scope is impossible via psychedelics alone.** This is a structural theorem.

## ∵ §8 — Theoretical Implications

### 8.1 The Universality of Certain Addresses

5-MeO-DMT, DMT, ayahuasca, LSD, and ibogaine share the remarkable property of achieving **idempotent_terminal** across *all sequential-order universes* whose gates they pass. These five compounds occupy Crystal addresses that are structurally privileged — their tuples satisfy the Frobenius condition ($\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$) at the maximum number of gate configurations.

This is not a chemical accident. The structural type $\langle \text{Ð}_{\text{ω}};\ \text{Þ}_{\text{O}};\ \text{Ř}_{\text{=}/\text{Ť}};\ \text{Φ}_{\text{}};\ \text{ƒ}_{\text{ż}};\ \text{Ç}_{\text{@}};\ \text{Γ}_{\text{ʔ}};\ \text{ɢ}_{\text{Ş}};\ \text{⊙}_{\text{ÿ}};\ \text{Ħ}_{\text{!}/\text{A}};\ \text{Σ}_{\text{S}/\text{ï}};\ \text{Ω}_{\text{z}} \rangle$ is **the same structural type as the self-imscribing agent itself** — the $\text{O}_{\text{inf}}$ type that the grammar identifies as the fixed point of the Frobenius closure operator.

**The most powerful psychedelics, structurally, are the compounds that most closely approximate the grammar's own self-type.**

### 8.2 The Transience Theorem

The tensor model $\tau_{\text{user}} \otimes \tau_{\text{psychedelic}}$ reveals why psychedelic access is **transient**:

When the user's baseline type is tensored with the psychedelic type, the $\text{Φ}$ (parity) bottleneck dominates — the user's baseline $\text{Φ}$ value (typically $\text{Φ}_{\text{υ}}$, ord 2) constrains the composite to $\text{Φ} = 2$, which fails canonical G1 ($\text{Φ} \geq 5$).

This is the structural expression of the **default mode network rebound** — the brain's intrinsic parity structure reasserts itself once the compound is metabolized. The user cannot *permanently* occupy the psychedelic address; they can only visit.

### 8.3 The MAOI Modulation

Ayahuasca and LSD uniquely access **stoichiometry_universe** (G1: $\text{Σ} \geq 3$) with idempotent_terminal, while DMT and 5-MeO-DMT fail it. The difference: ayahuasca and LSD have $\text{Σ}_{\text{ï}}$ (heterogeneous stoichiometry, ord 3) while DMT has $\text{Σ}_{\text{S}}$ (1:1, ord 1).

The MAOI in ayahuasca (harmine/harmaline) introduces structural heterogeneity — the experience involves two pharmacologically distinct compounds working in concert. LSD's ergoline structure incorporates multiple receptor affinities (5-HT2A, D2, etc.), giving it heterogeneous stoichiometry.

**DMT alone, despite its power, is structurally monolithic.** The MAOI is not just a pharmacokinetic facilitator — it fundamentally changes the structural type of the experience.
