\hypertarget{frobenius-algebra}{%
\subsection{Frobenius Algebra}\label{frobenius-algebra}}

\(\mu \circ \delta = \mathrm{id}\) (normalization) ---
\(\mu(\mu\otimes\mathrm{id}) = \mu(\mathrm{id}\otimes\mu)\) (assoc) ---
\((\delta\otimes\mathrm{id})\circ\delta = (\mathrm{id}\otimes\delta)\circ\delta\)
(coassoc) ---
\((\mu\otimes\mathrm{id})(\mathrm{id}\otimes\delta) = \delta\mu = (\mathrm{id}\otimes\mu)(\delta\otimes\mathrm{id})\)
(Frobenius).

\textbf{Spider Theorem:} all diagrams with same connectivity = same
morphism.
