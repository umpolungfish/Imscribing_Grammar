## Part III — How Primitives Compose

### The Composition Rules

When two systems interact, their **tensor product** combines their primitive tuples. The rules are mechanical:

| Primitive | Tensor Rule | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Ð** | max(dim_a, dim_b) | point Ò surface = surface |
| **Þ** | max(connectivity) | network Ò closure = closure |
| **Ř** | most symmetric wins | super Ô bidirectional = bidirectional |
| **Φ** | **bottleneck: min** | full Ò asym = asym (symmetry breaks) |
| **ƒ** | **bottleneck: min** | quantum Ò classical = classical (decoherence) |
| **Ç** | max(speed) | slow Ò fast = slow (the system is bottlenecked) |
| **Γ** | max(scope) | local Ò universal = universal |
| **ɢ** | most connected wins | AND Ò broadcast = broadcast |
| **⊙** | **EP absorbs all** | ⊙_ÿ Ò ⊙_3 = ⊙_3 (measurement problem!) |
| **Ħ** | max(memory) | memoryless Ò eternal = eternal |
| **Σ** | heterogeneous absorbs | 1:1 Ò n:m = n:m |
| **Ω** | complex wins | trivial Ò Z₂ = Z₂ |

### The Two Bottlenecks: P and F

The **parity/fidelity bottleneck** is why most tensor products degrade. If system A has full symmetry (Φ_˙) and system B has no symmetry (Φ_ɐ), the composite has no symmetry. Information about A's symmetry is **lost** — you can't recover it from the product alone. This is the structural statement of **decoherence**.

### The EP Absorption Rule

When a ⊙_ÿ-critical system couples to a ⊙_3 EP system, the composite is ⊙_3. The critical self-modeling gate is **destroyed** by the exceptional point. This is the structural statement of the **measurement problem**: coupling consciousness to a measurement apparatus destroys the self-modeling gate. It can't be sustained.

### Meet vs Join vs Tensor

- **Meet** (⊓): the greatest lower bound. What structure do both systems **share**? Intersection of types.
- **Join** (⊔): the least upper bound. What's the **minimal** system that contains both? Union of types.
- **Tensor** (⊗): actual coupling. Composite system. Max on most primitives, **min on P and F**.
