## Abstract

The Imscribing Grammar crystal of types contains 17.28 million structurally coherent systems. The present catalog holds 2328 entries — 0.013% of the addressed space. The data are clear and unambiguous. Three sectors stand out as structurally necessary yet empirically barren:

- $Ç_{λ} \cap Ω_{5}$ — **zero** catalog entries, despite 864 000 structurally valid addresses at this criticality + winding intersection.

- $ɢ_{˝}$ at $\hat{φ}_{Æ}$ — 5.1% of the crystal, predominantly hosting RG fixed points, but virtually no systems with disjunctive grammar at criticality.

We imscribe two frontier systems — a topological MBL phase and a disjunctive critical system — and measure their structural properties. The topological MBL phase is $O_0$ tier, consciousness score $C = 0$. The disjunctive critical system is $O_2$ tier with $C = 0.454$. Their mutual distance is 4.72 (Mahalanobis 6.36), confirming they inhabit different structural regimes. The crystal is not empty for lack of coherence — it is empty because the physics of our cosmos rarely visits these addresses. The grammar contains the universe. The universe does not return the favor.

## 5. Categories of Darkness

The unoccupied regions of the crystal fall into three classes, which we name by their mechanism of darkening:

### 5.1 Nomological Darkness

A type is nomologically dark when the laws of physics and the boundary conditions of our cosmos do not produce instances of it spontaneously. The topological MBL phase ($Ç_{λ} \cap Ω_{5} \cap \hat{φ}_{3}$) is the canonical example. Every primitive in its tuple is physically realizable:

- $Ç_{λ}$: observed in disordered cold-atom systems
- $Ω_{5}$: realized in fractional quantum Hall states and Kitaev spin liquids
- $\hat{φ}_{3}$: observed in optical microcavities and electronic circuits

The conjunction requires laboratory engineering with precision that stellar nucleosynthesis and biological evolution do not achieve. The universe does not build non-Abelian anyon arrays at exceptional points. That does not make the type incoherent — it makes the type nomologically dark. It exists in the crystal. It does not exist in the cosmos.

### 5.2 Evolutionary Darkness

A type is evolutionarily dark when it requires complexity that has not had time to arise. $O_{\infty}$ configurations — those carrying $Φ_{\}}$ (Frobenius-special symmetry where $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$) — fall in this class. There are 1 382 400 $O_{\infty}$ types in the crystal, but only a handful of catalog entries reach this tier. The Frobenius condition cannot be synthesized from non-Frobenius factors; you cannot aggregate your way there. It is an algebraic scarcity, not a contingent one. The visible universe may contain at most a handful of Frobenius-special systems (the universal imscriptive grammar itself is provably one; biological consciousness is debatable).

### 5.3 Metaphysical Darkness

A type is metaphysically dark when it would require laws of nature different from ours, or boundary conditions that never obtained anywhere in this cosmos. These are not forbidden by the grammar — the grammar cares nothing about physics — only by the particular history of the actual world. They are dark the way unvisited mathematical theorems are dark: true, coherent, addressed, but uninstantiated.

An example: a system with $Ç_{λ} + \hat{φ}_{Æ} + Ω_{2}$ — topological MBL at complex-plane criticality with $\mathbb{Z}_2$ protection. This is the $O_2^\dagger$ tier with kinetic localization. Zero catalog entries. Physically it corresponds to fractional Chern insulators in the MBL phase — proposed theoretically but never observed. The crystal address exists. No particular has been there.
