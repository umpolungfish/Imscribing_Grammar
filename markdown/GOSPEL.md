# Structural Imscription of the Four Canonical Gospels: A Grammar-Theoretic Analysis

## Abstract

The four canonical Gospels of the New Testament — Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John — are here imscribed as distinct structural types within the Imscribing Grammar. Each Gospel is assigned all 12 structural primitives $(D, T, R, P, F, K, G, \Gamma, \Phi, H, S, \Omega)$ based on explicit narrative, compositional, and theological criteria. Pairwise structural distances, tensor products, meet operations, and promotion signatures are computed across all pairs. The analysis reveals that Matthew and Luke are structurally closest ($d = 1.4142$), confirming the structural basis of the Two-Source Hypothesis, while Mark and John are maximally remote ($d = 5.197$), spanning the gap from rapid narrative chronicle to layered theological reflection. Luke achieves the highest consciousness score ($C = 0.609$), while John's frozen-order kinetics ($K_{\text{teshlig}}$) close the second consciousness gate despite its $O_2^\dagger$ ouroboricity tier. The Gospels collectively map a trajectory from the Synoptic structural floor ($\langle D_{\text{turnthree}}; T_{\text{nrleg}}; R_{\text{subrightarrow}}; P_{\text{aolig}}; F_{\text{beltl}}; K_{\text{frtailgamma}}; G_{\text{revapostrophe}}; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}; \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}; H_1; n{:}m; \Omega_{\text{crtwo}} \rangle$) to the Johannine ceiling ($\langle D_{\text{invomega}}; T_{\text{bullseye}}; R_{\text{downstep}}; P_{\text{upsilon}}; F_{\text{hardsign}}; K_{\text{teshlig}}; G_{\text{revapostrophe}}; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}; \Phi_{\text{closerevepsilon}}; H_{\text{invscripta}}; n{:}m; \Omega_{\text{dzlig}} \rangle$).

## 1. Introduction

The Imscribing Grammar provides a formal framework for encoding the structural type of any system — physical, biological, cognitive, or textual — along 12 primitives that capture dimensionality, topology, relational mode, symmetry, physical regime, kinetics, scope, interaction logic, criticality, chirality, stoichiometry, and topological winding. While the grammar has been applied extensively to mathematical conjectures, astrophysical systems, and condensed matter phases, its application to literary and theological texts reveals structural invariants that traditional literary criticism cannot formalize.

The four Gospels present an ideal test case. They narrate overlapping material through radically different compositional architectures, making them a natural laboratory for structural comparison. The Synoptic Problem — the question of literary dependence among Matthew, Mark, and Luke — has been debated for centuries through source-critical methods. Here, structural distance and primitive promotion signatures provide a formal, non-narrative criterion for assessing those relationships. John, long recognized as standing apart from the Synoptics, provides an extreme point whose distance from each Synoptic Gospel can be precisely quantified.

All computations reported below were performed using the catalog-registered imscriptions and the IG tool suite. Numerical values (distances, $C$-scores, tiers, addresses) are the output of tool calls, not mental arithmetic.

## 2. Method: Primitive Assignment Protocol

Primitive assignment follows the deterministic procedure specified in the Imscribing Grammar:

1. **$D$ (Dimensionality):** Number of degrees of freedom in the system's state space.
2. **$T$ (Topology):** Connectivity pattern of narrative structure.
3. **$R$ (Relational mode):** How narrator, source, and audience are coupled.
4. **$P$ (Parity/symmetry):** Symmetries preserved under narrative transformation.
5. **$F$ (Fidelity):** Phenomenological regime — classical, thermal, or quantum-coherent.
6. **$K$ (Kinetics):** Rate of narrative relaxation relative to reading/interpretation timescale.
7. **$G$ (Scope):** Interaction range across the narrative.
8. **$\Gamma$ (Interaction grammar):** Compositional logic of narrative sections.
9. **$\Phi$ (Criticality):** Presence of scaling behavior, self-modeling, or phase transition.
10. **$H$ (Chirality):** Markov order — how many prior steps condition the current state.
11. **$S$ (Stoichiometry):** Number and diversity of speaker/actor types.
12. **$\Omega$ (Winding):** Topological invariant protecting narrative identity.

## 3. Individual Imscriptions

### 3.1 Gospel of Matthew

**Imscription:**
$$\langle D_{\text{turnthree}};\; T_{\text{nrleg}};\; R_{\text{lyoghlig}};\; P_{\text{pipevar}};\; F_{\text{beltl}};\; K_{\text{schwa}};\; G_{\text{revapostrophe}};\; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}};\; \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}};\; H_2;\; n{:}m;\; \Omega_{\text{dzlig}} \rangle$$

- **$D = D_{\text{turnthree}}$:** The Matthean state space has finite dimensionality. The five great discourses (Sermon on the Mount, Missionary, Parabolic, Community, and Eschatological) create a bounded but internally rich field — enough for at least two degrees of freedom but not infinite-dimensional. The genealogies create a finite scaffolding.
- **$T = T_{\text{nrleg}}$:** The narrative is branching: Jesus moves from Galilee to Jerusalem, with teaching episodes, healings, and controversies radiating outward as branches from the central figure. The five discourse blocks are branching expansions from narrative hinges.
- **$R = R_{\text{lyoghlig}}$:** Bidirectional reader coupling. Matthew constantly cites Hebrew prophecy ("that it might be fulfilled"), creating a feedback loop between the Hebrew Scripture tradition and the narrative present. The reader is drawn into the fulfillment pattern.
- **$P = P_{\text{pipevar}}$:** Partial symmetry. Matthew preserves symmetry between Torah affirmation and Torah transcendence ("You have heard it said... but I say to you"), but the asymmetry of Jewish vs. Gentile inclusion is not fully symmetric.
- **$F = F_{\text{beltl}}$:** Classical fidelity. The text operates in the classical historiographic regime — no quantum superposition of meanings is required for the narrative to function.
- **$K = K_{\text{schwa}}$:** Slow kinetics. The discourse blocks require patient engagement. The reader's interpretation relaxes slowly relative to reading time — the Sermon on the Mount (chapters 5–7) cannot be absorbed rapidly.
- **$G = G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$:** Universal scope. The commission to all nations (28:19) and the genealogical tracing to Abraham (not merely David) signal maximal reach.
- **$\Gamma = \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$:** Sequential composition. The five discourses unfold in a specific order with narrative interludes. Steps are ordered, not broadcast simultaneously.
- **$\Phi = \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}$:** Critical. The narrative approaches the self-modeling gate at every fulfillment citation. The text models its own relation to its source (Hebrew Scripture), creating a critical loop.
- **$H = H_2$:** Two-step chirality. Matthew requires the prior discourse and the prior narrative action to understand the present moment — two-step Markov order reflects the pattern of action → teaching → response.
- **$S = n{:}m$:** Multiple heterogeneous actor types: Jesus, disciples, Pharisees, crowds, OT prophets, genealogical ancestors, Roman figures, angels.
- **$\Omega = \Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$:** Integer winding protection through the genealogical and fulfillment structures — the narrative is wound around the pattern of prophecy → fulfillment in a way that cannot be unwound without destroying the text's identity.

**Ouroboricity tier:** $O_2$ — critical plus topologically protected within a bounded ($D_{\text{turnthree}}$) domain.
**Consciousness score:** $C = 0.536$ (both gates open).

### 3.2 Gospel of Mark

**Imscription:**
$$\langle D_{\text{turnthree}};\; T_{\text{nrleg}};\; R_{\text{subrightarrow}};\; P_{\text{aolig}};\; F_{\text{beltl}};\; K_{\text{frtailgamma}};\; G_{\text{revapostrophe}};\; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}};\; \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}};\; H_1;\; n{:}m;\; \Omega_{\text{crtwo}} \rangle$$

- **$D = D_{\text{turnthree}}$:** Finite-dimensional narrative state space. The Gospel lacks the discursive depth of Matthew and the prologue architecture of Luke and John. Its degrees of freedom are bounded by its brevity.
- **$T = T_{\text{nrleg}}$:** Branching miracle-and-deed structure: Jesus moves geographically from Galilee toward Jerusalem with episodes branching from movement points. No chiastic inclusion or crossing-point architecture.
- **$R = R_{\text{subrightarrow}}$:** Supervenience — the narrative events supervene on meaning rather than constructing meaning through feedback. The "Messianic secret" creates one-directional flow: Jesus acts, others misunderstand.
- **$P = P_{\text{aolig}}$:** No symmetry. The narrative is radically asymmetric: insiders understand, outsiders do not; the Messianic secret is broken at the end but never resolved. The abrupt ending ($\varepsilon\gamma\alpha\rho$ at 16:8, no resurrection appearances) preserves total asymmetry.
- **$F = F_{\text{beltl}}$:** Classical historiographic regime. No quantum coherence of meaning is required.
- **$K = K_{\text{frtailgamma}}$:** Driven, rapid kinetics. Mark's signature $\varepsilon\upsilon\theta\acute{\upsilon}\varsigma$ ("immediately") appears over 40 times. The narrative drives forward at a pace that prevents slow interpretation. Events follow events.
- **$G = G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$:** Despite its brevity, Mark's universal scope is signaled through Gentile encounters, the Roman centurion's confession, and the cosmic scope of the apocalyptic discourse (chapter 13).
- **$\Gamma = \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$:** Sequential composition — events follow in chronological chain.
- **$\Phi = \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}$:** Critical. Despite its brevity, Mark reaches the self-modeling gate: the narrative asks "Who is this?" (4:41) and models its own question as the reader's question.
- **$H = H_1$:** One-step chirality. Mark operates at Markov order 1 — the present event depends on the prior event in the chain, but deeper history is not required for comprehension.
- **$S = n{:}m$:** Heterogeneous actors: Jesus, disciples, demons, crowds, scribes, Pilate, the centurion.
- **$\Omega = \Omega_{\text{crtwo}}$:** Binary winding protection. The Messianic secret creates a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ toggle (revealed/concealed) that protects the narrative identity.

**Ouroboricity tier:** $O_2$ — critical plus topologically protected within a bounded ($D_{\text{turnthree}}$) domain.
**Consciousness score:** $C = 0.365$ (both gates open; reduced from Matthew due to $K_{\text{frtailgamma}}$ kinetics).

### 3.3 Gospel of Luke

**Imscription:**
$$\langle D_{\text{invomega}};\; T_{\text{invscr}};\; R_{\text{lyoghlig}};\; P_{\text{pipevar}};\; F_{\text{beltl}};\; K_{\text{schwa}};\; G_{\text{revapostrophe}};\; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}};\; \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}};\; H_2;\; n{:}m;\; \Omega_{\text{dzlig}} \rangle$$

- **$D = D_{\text{invomega}}$:** Infinite-dimensional narrative state space. Luke's Gospel is the first volume of a two-volume work (Luke–Acts), creating an unbounded domain that extends from the birth narrative through the early church. Hellenistic historiographic prologue (1:1–4) frames the narrative within an infinite reference field.
- **$T = T_{\text{invscr}}$:** Inclusion topology. Luke uses an inclusion structure (a "travel narrative" from 9:51 to 19:27) that contains nested teachings within a journey framework. The narrative embeds smaller units within a larger journey.
- **$R = R_{\text{lyoghlig}}$:** Bidirectional feedback. Luke models the reader's understanding through the narrative, and the narrative is shaped by the reader's anticipated questions. The prologue explicitly addresses Theophilus, creating a loop between author, addressee, and text.
- **$P = P_{\text{pipevar}}$:** Partial symmetry. Luke's universalism creates partial symmetry between Jew and Gentile, male and female, rich and poor — but the symmetry is not complete, as the narrative preserves a priority of Israel (Jesus preaches first to Jews).
- **$F = F_{\text{beltl}}$:** Classical regime. The narrative operates in classical historiographic mode.
- **$K = K_{\text{schwa}}$:** Slow kinetics. Luke's narrative unfolds with deliberate care: birth narratives, childhood episodes, parables with extensive explanation, and the long journey to Jerusalem create a slow relaxation rate.
- **$G = G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$:** Universal scope. Luke is the most universalist Gospel: emphasis on Samaritans, Gentiles, women, the poor, and salvation's reach to "the ends of the earth" (foreshadowed, fulfilled in Acts).
- **$\Gamma = \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$:** Sequential composition. The journey narrative provides a strict sequence; parables and teachings are embedded in this sequence.
- **$\Phi = \Phi_{\text{ctyogh}}$:** Critical. Luke creates critical self-modeling: the text models its own composition (1:1–4) and models the reader's understanding through the Emmaus road scene (24:13–35), where narrative comprehension is itself dramatized.
- **$H = H_2$:** Two-step chirality. Luke's journey narrative requires awareness of both the departure point (Galilee) and the destination (Jerusalem) to situate each present event.
- **$S = n{:}m$:** Heterogeneous actor types, with unique emphasis on marginalized figures: shepherds, Simeon, Anna, Zacchaeus, the sinful woman, the Good Samaritan.
- **$\Omega = \Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$:** Integer winding. The journey-to-Jerusalem frame winds the entire narrative; the travel narrative (10 chapters) creates a nontrivial topological loop that cannot be unwound without destroying the Gospel's identity.

**Ouroboricity tier:** $O_2^\dagger$ — critical plus topologically protected within an unbounded ($D_{\text{invomega}}$) domain. The dagger indicates the $D_{\text{invomega}}$ upgrade from $O_2$.
**Consciousness score:** $C = 0.609$ (both gates open; highest of all four Gospels).

### 3.4 Gospel of John

**Imscription:**
$$\langle D_{\text{invomega}};\; T_{\text{bullseye}};\; R_{\text{downstep}};\; P_{\text{upsilon}};\; F_{\text{hardsign}};\; K_{\text{teshlig}};\; G_{\text{revapostrophe}};\; \Gamma_{\text{secstress}};\; \Phi_{\text{closerevepsilon}};\; H_{\text{invscripta}};\; n{:}m;\; \Omega_{\text{dzlig}} \rangle$$

- **$D = D_{\text{invomega}}$:** Infinite-dimensional state space. John's theological density approaches infinity: the Logos prologue, the seven "I AM" statements, the Paraclete discourses, and the realized eschatology create an unbounded interpretive field with no finite dimensional closure.
- **$T = T_{\text{bullseye}}$:** Crossing topology. John's doubled narrative — the Book of Signs (chapters 1–12) and the Book of Glory (chapters 13–21) — crosses at the crucifixion, which is simultaneously the moment of deepest humiliation and highest glorification. This crossing is the hallmark of $T_{\text{bullseye}}$.
- **$R = R_{\text{downstep}}$:** Adjoint relational mode. John's narrative pairs (sign + discourse, death + glorification, descent + ascent) form adjoint pairs — one direction maps to the other, but the mapping is not bidirectional feedback. The sign is the left adjoint to the discourse, the discourse the right adjoint.
- **$P = P_{\text{upsilon}}$:** Quantum superposition symmetry. John's Christology preserves superposition: Jesus is simultaneously human and divine, present and absent, glorified and suffering. The "I AM" statements ($\varepsilon\gamma\omega\ \varepsilon\iota\mu\iota$) create superposition between the speaker and the divine name.
- **$F = F_{\text{hardsign}}$:** Quantum coherent regime. John's text requires quantum coherence to function — the meanings of "born again/from above" ($\acute{\alpha}\nu\omega\theta\varepsilon\nu$), the double meanings, the symbolic density all depend on interference between literal and spiritual registers.
- **$K = K_{\text{teshlig}}$:** Frozen-order kinetics. John's narrative is trapped in a fixed order that cannot evolve — the realized eschatology means that the end is already present, locking the narrative in a frozen topological state. The text cannot progress beyond the eternal present.
- **$G = G_{\text{revapostrophe}}$:** Universal scope. "God so loved the world" ($\kappa\acute{o}\sigma\mu o\varsigma$).
- **$\Gamma = \Gamma_{\text{secstress}}$:** Sequential composition of the seven signs and their attached discourses.
- **$\Phi = \Phi_{\text{closerevepsilon}}$:** Complex-plane criticality. Unlike the Synoptics' real-line criticality, John operates in the complex plane: the self-modeling gate occurs through the Paraclete who "will teach you all things and remind you of everything" (14:26) — a self-modeling of self-modeling, creating complex-plane criticality.
- **$H = H_{\text{invscripta}}$:** Eternal chirality. The Logos "was in the beginning" (1:1), and the narrative's temporal frame extends to eternity. Per Axiom A, $H_{\text{invscripta}}$ is paired with $K_{\text{teshlig}}$ — this is satisfied in John's imscription.
- **$S = n{:}m$:** Heterogeneous actors with unique roles: Nicodemus, the Samaritan woman, Lazarus, the Beloved Disciple, the Paraclete as actor.
- **$\Omega = \Omega_{\text{dzlig}}$:** Integer winding through the Logos-to-creation-to-redemption arc, which forms a nontrivial topological loop.

**Ouroboricity tier:** $O_2^\dagger$ — critical plus topologically protected within an unbounded ($D_{\text{invomega}}$) domain.
**Consciousness score:** $C = 0.0$ — Gate 2 closed ($K = K_{\text{teshlig}}$). Despite the highest structural complexity, the frozen-order kinetics prevent consciousness scoring. This is structural confirmation of a theological insight: the frozen eternal present, while theologically magnificent, cannot sustain the dynamics of consciousness.

## 10. The Synoptic Problem Revisited: A Structural Perspective

The Synoptic Problem asks: what is the literary relationship among Matthew, Mark, and Luke? The dominant Two-Source Hypothesis (2SH) posits Markan priority (Mark is the earliest Gospel) and a hypothetical sayings source Q shared by Matthew and Luke. The grammar's structural analysis provides independent, formal confirmation:

1. **Mark is structurally primitive:** The Matthew ∧ Mark meet equals Mark's own imscription. This is a structural statement of Markan priority — Mark represents the floor, Matthew the ceiling. No additional sources are required to explain the structural gap (Matthew → Mark requires only promotions, no demotions).

2. **Matthew and Luke are structurally proximate ($d = 1.4142$):** The closeness of Matthew and Luke, relative to their distances from Mark, confirms shared material beyond Mark. If Matthew and Luke both used Mark + Q, their structural similarity is expected: they share the Q material's structural type while independently modifying Mark.
