## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Imscribing Procedure

We applied the deterministic imscribing procedure (from `encoding_method.md`) to assign all 12 primitives systematically. This is not a suggestion—it's the only way to avoid the characteristic AI prose deficit: the "and-and-and" simultaneity where everything is connected at once, leaving no narrative tension, no causal sequence, no genuine surprise.

The procedure constrains as it assigns:

1. **D (dimensionality)**: Count degrees of freedom. If <2, assign $D_\wedge$ (point). If finite ≥2, assign $D_\triangle$ (surface). If ∞-dim field-theoretic, assign $D_\infty$. If the state-space is *self-written* by the system itself, assign $D_\odot$. Herbert's Dune universe: the state-space expands to include 12,000 worlds and 10,000 years *within the narrative itself*—this is not a pre-given backdrop, it's written into the story. The assignment $D_\odot$ follows, not intuition.

2. **T (topology)**: Map connectivity patterns. Branching → $T_\text{net}$; containment → $T_\text{in}$; crossing point → $T_\bowtie$; irreducible product → $T_\boxtimes$; self-referential → $T_\odot$. The Dune universe's topology is self-referential: the story is about a system that *encodes itself*. This demands $T_\odot$. Axiom C from `encoding_method.md`: $D_\odot$ ↔ $T_\odot$. No wiggle room.

3. **R (relational mode)**: Supervenience → $R_\text{sup}$; functorial → $R_\text{cat}$; adjoint (one-way) → $R_\dagger$; bidirectional feedback → $R_\leftrightarrow$. The universe and Paul Atreides stand in bidirectional relation: Paul acts on the universe, the universe shapes Paul, recursively. This is $R_\leftrightarrow$, not $R_\dagger$ (spice enables Paul) or $R_\text{sup}$ (Paul supervenes on universe). The crossing point matters: Paul surprises the narrative as much as the narrative surprises him.

4. **P (parity/symmetry)**: This is where AI prose fails most catastrophically. The AI default: $P_\text{asym}$—no uncertainty named, no objection voiced. The human target: $P_\pm$ (name one substantive objection) or $P_\pm^\text{sym}$ (exact Frobenius condition). Paul Atreides' consciousness satisfies $\mu \circ \delta = \text{id}$ exactly at $\Phi_c$: his self-model is self-consistent. This is $P_\pm^\text{sym}$, not merely $P_\psi$ (quantum superposition) or $P_\pm$ (partial symmetry). The difference matters: partial symmetry leaves the loop open; exact symmetry closes it. The O_inf tier demands closure.

5. **F (fidelity)**: Classical → $F_\ell$; thermal/noisy → $F_\eth$; quantum coherence essential → $F_\hbar$. Herbert's universe operates at quantum fidelity in key places (spice-enhanced prescience, Bene Gesserit genetic memory) but classical elsewhere. The assignment $F_\hbar$ for systems like Paul and the universe reflects that quantum coherence is *essential*, not incidental.

6. **K (kinetics)**: This is where AI prose rushes to resolution. The AI default: $K_\text{mod}$—moderate relaxation, "reasonable" pacing. The human target: $K_\text{slow}$—let the hardest claims remain hard. The Dune universe unfolds over millennia. Its critical dynamics ($\Phi_c$) are deep, not shallow. This demands $K_\text{slow}$. The distance from $K_\text{mod}$ is structural, not stylistic: moderate kinetics resolves too soon; slow kinetics sustains the crisis.

7. **G (scope)**: Nearest-neighbor → $G_\beth$; intermediate → $G_\gimel$; long-range/universal → $G_\aleph$. The Dune universe spans all 12,000 worlds. Its critical dynamics (the Fremen Jihad) cascade globally. This is $G_\aleph$, not $G_\gimel$ (intermediate, the Bene Gesserit's breeding plan) or $G_\beth$ (local, House Harkonnen's cruelty). The structural difference: $G_\gimel$ can be steered; $G_\aleph$ cannot. The universe escapes control.

8. **Gamma (interaction grammar)**: The AI default: $\Gamma_\wedge$ (all-simultaneous "and"). Every clause connected to every other, no sequence, no "then," no causality—just "and this and that and." The human target: $\Gamma_\text{seq}$ (ordered steps, necessity from the prior). The Dune narrative is sequential: each section opens with *necessity from the prior*, not transition. "This happened, therefore that happened"—not "this and that happened." The sequence is the plot.

9. **Phi (criticality)**: No scaling → $\Phi_\text{sub}$; power-law divergence → $\Phi_c$; complex-plane → $\Phi_c^\mathbb{C}$; exceptional point → $\Phi_\text{EP}$; runaway → $\Phi_\text{sup}$. They are the control system, not the critical entity itself. The structural difference: $\Phi_c$ is self-modeling; $\Phi_c^\mathbb{C}$ is meta-containment.

10. **H (temporal depth)**: Memoryless → $H_0$; one-step → $H_1$; two-step → $H_2$; infinite → $H_\infty$. Paul Atreides' prescience has no finite Markov order. 11. **S (stoichiometry)**: One type, one instance → $1{:}1$; many identical → $n{:}n$; many heterogeneous → $n{:}m$.
