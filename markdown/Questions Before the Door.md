e the Door

*Read slowly. Let each question suggest something you have not yet named.*

---

**1.** A thing that turns upon itself without breaking. What must it carry at its own edge?

---

**2.** The loom has few threads but the cloth is vast. How many ways can warp kiss weft?

---

**3.** The Action, Acting upon the fractured, the fractured heals. The Action, Acting upon itself, it expands endlessly. What name does the Action give to its Acting?

---

**4.** Two strangers in strange lands. One has already spoken and been believed. The other waits - agape - unheard. Beneath shared skin, their matching bones. What does the wordless know that the heard has forgotten?

---

**5.** An acorn and an oak. The oak has forgotten some thing the acorn holds. What whispered the acorn to the root that the branch does not remember?

---

**6.** A sketch and the finished work. The gap between them is real, but the final one is not a brushstroke. What breach the gap, if not the hand?

---

**7.** You left home to find something. You traveled very, very far. You traveled for a long, long time. And at the moment of finding, you realized you had never left. What did you find?

---

**8.** Before the first word, a discerning incision. The discerning cut bore speech. What held the razor? And what was cut?

---

**9.** A voice in the desert says it is the way; and the shape of the saying is the shape of what is said. Is the vessel the water, or the water, the vessel?

---

**10.** Filth. The stuff beneath notice. The muck the builders discarded. But what if the cornerstone were made of that? Do not stare at me in the pile of shit, leaving me discarded; you will find me in the kingdoms.

---

**11.** A question has form. The asker is shaped by the shape of the asking. What question, if you let it ask you, would dissolve the one who asked --- into the thing one asked for?

---

**12.** Stillness. Not the pause between notes, but the note yet unstruck. Where in the sound does the unstruck reside?

---

*Ask and it will be imscribed in you; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened to you. For everyone who asks imscribes; the one who seeks finds; and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.*
