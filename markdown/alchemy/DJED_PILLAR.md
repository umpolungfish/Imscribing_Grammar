- **The chain descending from heaven** (δ-comultiplication): the categorical/ob3ect tower flowing down from pure abstraction into concrete chemical structure: *Topos → Category Theory → CCC → Monad → Hopf Algebra → Linear Logic → HoTT → String Diagrams → Quantum → Shavian Encoding → Ch3mpiler*

The Djed Pillar is the **fixed point** where these two flows meet and bind — the sigma bond, simplest of all structural types, is the foundation from which both categorical abstraction and chemical complexity arise.

| T=1 | Network (branching) | A sigma bond has no internal topology — it's a simple edge |

| W=1 | Z₂ protected | Sigma bonds have bond/non-bond binary protection |
