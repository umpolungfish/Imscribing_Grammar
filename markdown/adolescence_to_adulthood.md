**Use either Albert Bandura's Social Learning or Lev Vygotsky's Sociocultural theory to explain influences or motivations for your choice of school major or career goal?**

Using Albert Bandura's Social Learning Theory, my decision to become an elementary school teacher was influenced a lot by the people I observed growing up. I remember watching my teachers and how they interacted with students, especially how patient they were and how they made learning feel more engaging instead of stressful. That really stuck with me and made me want to do something similar. Social Learning Theory explains that people learn by observing and imitating others, and in my case, seeing those positive role models played a big role in shaping my interest in working with young children.
