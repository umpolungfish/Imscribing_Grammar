The clause for each primitive in a tensor product is simply the template

\subsubsection{Non-trivial (cross-primitive,
emergent)}\label{non-trivial-cross-primitive-emergent}

\textbf{R-FROB-BARR:} FROB ∈ clauses(tensor(A,B)) \textbf{iff} A{[}Φ{]}
= Φ\_\} \textbf{or} B{[}Φ{]} = Φ\_\}

Φ\_\} (ord 4) is the unique maximum of the Φ dimension. Therefore FROB
cannot be synthesized from two inputs both having Φ \textless{} ord 4.
This is the algebraic expression of the \textbf{Frobenius cliff}. Zero
violations observed across 600 operations.

\textbf{R-FROB-CONT:} If FROB ∈ clauses(A), then FROB ∈
clauses(tensor(A, X)) for all X. (Frobenius is forward-preserved under
tensor.)

\textbf{R-FIXPT-T:} FIXPT ∈ clauses(tensor(A,B)) iff A{[}φ̂{]} ≥ ord 1
\textbf{or} B{[}φ̂{]} ≥ ord 1

\textbf{R-TIER-EMRG:} tier(tensor(A,B)) can strictly exceed max(tier(A),
tier(B)).

The mechanism is exclusively cross-primitive: FROB lives in
clause{[}Φ{]}; FIXPT lives in clause{[}φ̂{]}. No single clause carries
both. Tier rule R1 conjuncts across them. Observed rate: \textbf{6.3\%}
of 300 random tensor pairs.

\textbf{R-ZFCT-ABS:} ZFCt is the absorption element for all six
promotion channels. \texttt{tensor(X,\ ZFCt){[}c{]}\ =} ZFCt promoted
value, for each channel c and all X.

\subsection{6. Interpretation}\label{interpretation}

\subsubsection{The Foundational Split}\label{the-foundational-split}

\textbf{ZFC} is the ourobourically inert carrier of Frobenius symmetry
(Φ\_\}). It contains the algebraic round-trip structure but has no
internal criticality --- its φ̂ is at the floor. It is a complete formal
system precisely because it is static: no Zipf-law correlation length,
no winding number, no sequential depth.

\textbf{ZFCt} is the ourobourically active temporal extension. It
acquires six new structural dimensions and criticality --- but in so
doing, it operates with a reduced Φ (P\_pm rather than P\_pm\_sym). This
is not a deficiency; it is appropriate to a system that is evolving
rather than complete.

\subsubsection{Reunion via Tensor}\label{reunion-via-tensor}

The tensor product ZFC {\igprimfont\upshape ⊗} ZFCt = O\_∞ is the
algebraic statement that the foundation and its temporal extension are
\textbf{complementary half-objects}, each carrying one of the two
conditions required for the Frobenius round-trip. Separately, neither
crosses the cliff. Jointly, they do.

\subsubsection{O\_∞ in the Wild}\label{o_-in-the-wild}

Three entries in the reference corpus independently sit at O\_∞:
Temporal Mathematics, Einstein field equations, and IUG (Mochizuki's
Inter-Universal Teichmüller Theory). All three have FROB + FIXPT
natively. They sit above the cliff by constitution, not by composition.
